Title: Afghanistan Journalism Enhancement Education Program
Type: HTML

The Afghanistan Journalism Education Enhancement Program (AJEEP) is funded by the
U.S State Department to modernize journalism education in Afghanistan. This grant
is a partnership between San Jose State University and two Afghan universities, Balkh
University in the city of Mazar i Sharif and Herat University in the city of Herat.
