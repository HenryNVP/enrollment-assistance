Training and Development
San José State offers a broad range of learning opportunities for employees, including a series of courses for campus managers.
These opportunities are presented through:
- In-person workshops
- Seminars
- Online resources and tools.
Center for Faculty Development
The Center for Faculty Development supports the educational mission of the University by providing opportunities for growth and renewal for faculty in all realms of their professional lives and throughout the stages of their careers.
The Center strives to create a campus culture that values intellectual inquiry and scholarly and creative engagement in its many forms.
eLearning Options:
CSULearn
The California State University's eLearning system; houses all system-required training.
Go2Knowledge
Video and interactive learning options; free to all employees.
LinkedIn Learning
LinkedIn's eLearning platform; contains hundreds of video learning options; free to all employees.
