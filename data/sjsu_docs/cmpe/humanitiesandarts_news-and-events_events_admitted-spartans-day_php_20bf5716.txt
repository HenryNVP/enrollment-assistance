Department of Design
Department of Design Open House
Below you will find information from our design programs about their Admitted Spartan Day open houses that will be held between 11:00 - 2:00 PM. The department office is located in Room 120 of the Art & Design building should you have any questions or need help finding your way. We’ll also have some goodies there, so please stop by and say hello!
Welcome to the Animation & Illustration Program
Please RSVP here to attend the Animation & Illustration presentation. Arrive at the Science Building (SCI) and proceed to Room 142 between 11:00 AM and 12:30 PM (doors open at 10:30 AM). Immediately following the presentation, in-person guests can join us to tour the facilities and meet with students and faculty in the Art & Design building, 2nd floor.
Guests joining on Zoom should contact Program Coordinator, David Chai at david.chai@sjsu.edu for a link. Links will be shared with Zoom attendees on Friday, 4/17.
Welcome to the Graphic Design Program
Please arrive at the Art & Design building and proceed to Room 133 at 11:00 AM for a presentation with Graphic Design faculty (Marjan Khatibi and Cindy Raspiller). After the presentation, attendees will have a chance to ask questions and meet the faculty.
Welcome to the Industrial Design Program
Please arrive at the Art & Design building and proceed to Room 205 between 12:00 and 12:30 PM for a presentation with Industrial Design faculty. After the presentation, attendees will have a chance to tour the facilities and meet with faculty and students.
All About Interior Design & Interior Architecture
Please arrive at the Industrial Studies building to Room 241 between 12:00 and 12:30 PM. Volunteers will help seat everyone, and then the main presentation led by Interior Design faculty will begin promptly at 12:30 PM. After the presentation, in-person attendees will have a chance to tour the facilities and meet with faculty and students.
Waste to Wonder Exhibition | Art & Design Building, Room 214
