Books, Articles and Informational Resources
The legendary novelist and activist James Baldwin once wrote: “Not everything that is faced can be changed. But nothing can be changed until it is faced.” One of the ways we can face systemic racism at SJSU is through learning. Fiction, non-fiction, in-depth analysis, poetry, film, theater, and a variety of other platforms can help us to learn and understand systemic racism. A wide variety of resources are available on- and off-campus and are available to all who are interested, regardless of race, sex, color, ethnicity or national origin.
