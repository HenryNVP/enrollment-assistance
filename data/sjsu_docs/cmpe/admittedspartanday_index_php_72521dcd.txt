Admitted Spartan Day 2026
Saturday, April 18th, will be an exciting day packed with opportunities to connect with departments and campus partners, and discover more about becoming a Spartan! Registration information will be emailed to all newly admitted students. For questions about the event, email us at admittedspartan@sjsu.edu
Join us for a special welcome event at 8:00 a.m. in the Provident Credit Union Event Center. Hear from University leaders as they welcome you to the campus and share their vision for San Jose State University. We will also provide a general overview of the day's agenda, program offerings, and what to expect at Admitted Spartan Day.
