National Day of Racial Healing - SJSU Activities
The National Day of Racial Healing is a time to contemplate our shared values and create the blueprint together for #HowWeHeal from the effects of racism. Launched in January of 2017, it is an opportunity to bring ALL people together in their common humanity and inspire collective action to create a more just and equitable world. This will be SJSU's fourth year participating as a part of this larger nation-wide initiative. The National Day of Racial Healing is is open and available to all who are interested to participate, regardless of race, sex, color, ethnicity or national origin.
