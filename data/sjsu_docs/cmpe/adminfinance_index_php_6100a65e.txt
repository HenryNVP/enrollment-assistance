What We Do
San José State University Administration and Finance advances the university mission through several core operational departments ranging from business services, to facilities, to campus safety, and others. Guided by responsible stewardship principles of university resources and assets, Administration and Finance helps guide Silicon Valley's public university growth as a hub for world-changing learning, research and innovation.

SJSU's new Vice President for Admin and Finance
Kathryn Kaoudis joins San José State University as the next vice president for administration and finance and chief financial officer, beginning September 1, 2025.
Spartan Village on the Paseo Welcomes Latest Cohort of Residents
SJSU accomplished a landmark private-public partnership to transform a former luxury hotel tower into a 700-bed residence hall that opened in August 2024. New environmental design installed on the skybridge in August 2025 welcomes the second cohort of student residents.
SJSU Campus Master Plan Approved by CSU
The latest iteration of the Campus Master Plan, the first since 2001, guides the university's physical growth and development through 2040. The plan was approved by the CSU Board of Trustees in May 2025.
