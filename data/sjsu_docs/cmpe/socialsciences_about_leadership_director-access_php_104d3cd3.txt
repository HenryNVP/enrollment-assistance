Joel Morales
Director of the College of Social Sciences Student Success Center
Joel Morales was appointed as the Director of the Academic Counseling Center for the College of Social Sciences Student Success Center in March 2020.
Joel graduated with his Bachelor of Arts in Intensive Psychology from University of California Santa Cruz and his Master of Science in Counseling from California State University Bakersfield. His previous roles include human resources and coordinator for job and internship placement for a community college. He is enthusiastic about helping students find their passions, develop their interests, and succeed in achieving their goals. Joel comes from Bakersfield, CA and is excited to apply his knowledge and skills in developing and enhancing programs at San Jose State University for student success.
