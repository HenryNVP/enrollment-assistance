Dean's Fund for Excellence
The College of Social Sciences Dean’s Fund for Excellence supports our students and faculty in the areas of teaching and learning, cutting edge research, and community engagement. The Dean’s Fund for Excellence helps us to create opportunities by providing our faculty, staff, and students with the resources they need to excel and lead. The Fund for Excellence offers critical support through flexible funding that can provide immediate use funds to support our programs, student success, faculty innovation and our many community partnerships.
