Academic Programs
Academic Programs is a support unit for students declared in a College of Science major, students seeking to apply to a science minor or major, or students requesting an equivalency review of a science course. Academic Programs also provides information and guidance for student internships, research, and career opportunities.
Please navigate to the Students webpage to find detailed information regarding:
- Advising
- Applying to a College of Science Major
- Career & Internship Support
- College of Science Minor Applications
- Course Equivalency Petitions
- Orientation
- Research Talks
- Student Scholarships
- Student Petitions
- Student Research & Research Support Programs
