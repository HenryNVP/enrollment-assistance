Road Map to Our Future
This is a campus of change agents and a community that thrives in the face of challenges. As the first public university in the West and the original startup in Silicon Valley, San José State has always been a place of firsts—driving innovation and shaping industries. From preparing teachers for the frontier to becoming a dynamic, comprehensive university at the epicenter of the future, we have evolved alongside the needs of our students, our region, and the world. Today, San José State is experiencing a transformational shift as a pre-eminent urban public university committed to academic excellence, student belonging and success. With a culture of vibrancy and optimism, we empower fearless forward-thinkers to lead, create, and solve global challenges.
Transformation 2030
Together - with Transformation 2030 as the blueprint - our university’s students, faculty, staff, alumni and other supporters are all helping to realize San José State’s position as a nationally prominent urban public university.
