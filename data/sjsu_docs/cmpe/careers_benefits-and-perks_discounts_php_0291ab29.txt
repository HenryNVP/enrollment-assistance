Discounts
Employees (other than student employees) are eligible for a number of discounts including:
- FREE admission to ALL Spartan Athletics home events
- Amusement Parks
- Cell Phone Service
- Hotels
- Movies
- Museums
- Rental Cars
- Software
See Employee Discounts for the full list of available discounts.
