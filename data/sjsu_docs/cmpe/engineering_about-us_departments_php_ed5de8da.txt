Departments
The SJSU College of Engineering comprises of ten departments and independent programs. The departments are overseen by a department chair, and may contain one or more areas of study. The independent programs are overseen by a director, and are generally smaller than departments.
- Aerospace Engineering
- Aviation and Technology
- Biomedical Engineering
- Chemical and Materials Engineering
- Civil and Environmental Engineering
- Computer Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Industrial and Systems Engineering
- Interdisciplinary Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Extended Studies
The degrees, minors, and certificate programs offered by each department car be found on our Programs page.
