SJSU students can borrow laptops, iPads, hotspots, and more for free from SCS, located on the 1st floor of the King Library at the Circulation Desk.
To check out equipment you must have a current Tower ID card. All SCS equipment loans(except semester-long laptops and tablets) are done in person and are on a first-come, first-served basis. Sorry, we can't hold or reserve equipment! See Lending Policies for more information.
