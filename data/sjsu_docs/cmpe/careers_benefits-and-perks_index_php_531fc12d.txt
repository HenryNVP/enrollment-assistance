Benefits and Perks
SJSU Takes Care of You
As employees of the California State University (CSU), you have a comprehensive program of health benefits including medical, dental, vision, flexible benefits programs and more available to you and, in many instances, your family. Additionally, you may be eligible to extend your health, dental and vision benefits to dependents up to age 26.
Medical, dental and vision coverage are available for most employees, with many options to ensure the best healthcare package for your needs.
Full time employees can vest for retirement in just 5 years! Working 10 years lets you retire with lifetime health benefits.
More than just sick and vacation; a variety of leave programs are available to employees.
Expand your knowledge through in-person workshops, online training, and more.
Take classes at any of the CSU's 23 campuses at a reduced rate or transfer the benefit to your eligible dependent.
Get discounts on phone service, hotels, rental cars, theme parks, movies, museums and more!
Find balance at work by attending a fitness class or taking a swim in the new Aquatic and Recreation Center.
