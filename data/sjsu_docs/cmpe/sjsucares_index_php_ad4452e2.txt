Title: SJSU Cares
Type: HTML

SJSU Cares is committed to fostering a campus environment rooted in equity, care, and belonging by providing a range of support, including immediate assistance and ongoing services, to address students’ basic needs and well-being. We partner with students to navigate and overcome barriers through holistic, personalized support, resources, and referrals. All enrolled students, including undergraduates, graduates, international students, and undocumented students, are eligible to access SJSU Cares programs and services.
