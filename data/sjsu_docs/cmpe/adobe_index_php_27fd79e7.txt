Title: Adobe Creative Campus
Type: HTML

San José State University has been designated as an Adobe Creative Campus. The Adobe Creative Campus program distinguishes leading colleges and universities that recognize the value of teaching digital literacy in higher education. Adobe plays an important role at San Jose State University, as our students, faculty and staff have access to the Adobe Creative Cloud program suite. Discover Adobe at SJSU, including creative classroom applications, events, learning opportunities, resources and the Adobe Creative Cloud.
