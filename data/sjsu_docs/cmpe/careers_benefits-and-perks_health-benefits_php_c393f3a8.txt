Health Benefits
The California State University offers its eligible employees one of the most comprehensive benefits packages on the market.
- Medical, dental and vision benefits for you and your family.
- Worth 30-35% of your base salary.
- Depending on your job, you may also be eligible for Accidental Death and Dismemberment, Term Life and/or Long Term Disability Insurance.
