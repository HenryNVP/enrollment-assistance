Change of Major Applications
In order to change a major into a Science major, please complete and submit a Change of Major Application by the submission deadline (see below). The applications are online and the links to the applications will only be available during the application period (see below). The applications will be reviewed after grades and academic standing posts for the current semester. Therefore, students will be notified of acceptance or denial several weeks into the next semester.
Submitting a Change of Major Application does not automatically result in being admitted to the major you are interested in. During the application process students should continue to make progress in their current major and ONLY take the courses needed for the change of major application.
Application period and deadline:
Nov. 1st at 12:00 am - Dec. 1st at 11:59 pm for the Spring semester
Apr. 1st at 12:00 am - May 1st at 11:59 pm for the Fall semester
