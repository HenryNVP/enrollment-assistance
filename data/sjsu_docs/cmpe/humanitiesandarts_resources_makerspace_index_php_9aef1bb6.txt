H&A Launch Pad
Welcome to the Launch Pad Makerspace!
The College of H&A is excited to welcome you to our Launch Pad Makerspace! Located in the Industrial Studies Building, Room 233, we're here to help you create in new and exciting ways. Come check out what we have to offer or just come hang out!
