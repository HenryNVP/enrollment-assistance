Leave Programs
As an SJSU employee you are eligible for:
- 13 paid holidays and 1 personal holiday each year.
- Accrual of vacation and sick hours each month.
- Additional leave programs should you need to be out of the office for an extended period of time.
See Leave Programs for more information.
