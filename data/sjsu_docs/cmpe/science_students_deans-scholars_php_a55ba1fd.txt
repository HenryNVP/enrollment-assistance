Congratulations Spring and Fall 2025 Honor Students!
College of Science students who have earned between a 3.65 and 3.99 GPA for the last spring and/or fall semester are Dean's Scholars.
Students who earned a 4.0 GPA for the last spring and/or fall semester are President's Scholars.
