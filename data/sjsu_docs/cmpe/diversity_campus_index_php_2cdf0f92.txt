Diversity @ SJSU
The Office of Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (ODEI): A part of the Office of the President, ODEI seeks to build individual, organizational, and institutional capacity to enact systemic change on issues of equity and social inequality. ODEI helps coordinate a number of institutional excellence initatives that all SJSU community members are welcome to be involved in, regardless of race, sex, color, ethnicity or national origin.
Chief Diversity Officer: Dr. Kristin Nicole Dukes serves as San José State University's Senior Diversity Officer since January of 2024.
belong@SJSU: in 2019 SJSU engaged in a campus climate for inclusion assessment. These efforts continue ongoing assessments of the campus climate.
