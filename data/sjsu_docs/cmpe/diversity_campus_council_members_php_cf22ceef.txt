Institutional Excellence Council Members
We are pleased to announce the members of the Institutional Excellence Council (IEC) members. This council will identify equity gaps and make recommendations to address systemic historical inequities, prioritizing equity for historically marginalized groups and issues related to improving campus climate through campus engagement, review of relevant data, reports and research. Council members also will share recommendations for presidential review.
