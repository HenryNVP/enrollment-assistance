Sense of Community Survey
The Sense of Community Survey (aka campus climate survey) asks students, faculty, staff, and administrators about the extent to which they feel a sense of community at SJSU. Information from this survey will be used to understand which experiences at SJSU promote or diminish the sense of community and belonging felt by students and employees.
