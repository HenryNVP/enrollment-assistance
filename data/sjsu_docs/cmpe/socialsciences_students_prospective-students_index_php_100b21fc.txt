Prospective Students
We are honored that you are considering joining us at the College of Social Sciences at San Jose State University!
Why Choose SJSU’s College of Social Sciences?
- Innovative Research & Applied Learning - Explore social inequalities, cultural diversity, and public policy through hands-on projects that equip you to tackle real-world challenges and drive change.
- Dedicated Faculty & Collaborative Environment - Thrive with expert mentors, small classes, and interdisciplinary research in a supportive, inclusive community.
- Prime Location & Community Engagement - Located in Silicon Valley, SJSU connects you with diverse communities, policymakers, and leading organizations, offering unmatched opportunities for internships, research, and community projects.
- Vibrant Student Life & Opportunities - Enjoy endless avenues for growth through student organizations, community projects, and study abroad programs that broaden your perspective and promote social progress.
Discover your passion and shape your future. With 12 departments offering over 70 undergraduate majors and minors, and 15+ graduate programs with certificates, we empower you to find the perfect fit for your academic goals. Explore our departments and programs and discover how we can help you achieve your academic and professional aspirations!
