Report an Accessibility Concern
San José State University is committed to accessibility and inclusion for all students, faculty, staff, and community members. Please use the following form to report any accessibility barriers related to campus facilities, buildings, and grounds; course materials and learning conditions; and web and IT applications and services.
Report an Accessibility Concern
To report an emergency, DO NOT use this form. Call University Police Department at (408) 924-2222, or dial 911.
