Career & Internship Support
Internships
Internships within the College of Science (CoS) offer invaluable opportunities to gain practical experience. Through internships, students can apply their classroom knowledge in real-world settings, enhancing their understanding of their chosen field and building essential skills. Internships provide hands-on learning, exposure to industry professionals, and a chance to work on meaningful projects. By participating in a College of Science internship, students can develop a competitive edge in their career pursuits and establish a strong foundation for future success.
Information about internship opportunities can be accessed through the College of Science Advising II for Juniors & Seniors on Canvas. If you do not have access to the College of Science Advising on Canvas, submit a request to be added to the College of Science Advising II Canvas course.
