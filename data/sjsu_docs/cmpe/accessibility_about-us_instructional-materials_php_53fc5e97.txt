Accessible Instructional Materials Committee
What do we do?
The Accessible Technology Initiative/Accessible Instructional Materials (ATI/AIM) committee is dedicated to staying up-to-date on current accessibility standards related to documents and media with a specific focus on course materials. Members research new accessible technologies and communicate best practices to the campus community through an annual report, campus events and outreach.
ATI/AIM Committee Event Promotion and Outreach
- Provides up-to-date information on current accessibility policies, including Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).
- Contributes to the Accessibility at SJSU biannual newsletter.
- Collaborates with other CSUs at the monthly UDOIT Common Interest Group (CIG) meetings.
- Connects with the University Council on Accessibility and Compliance (UCAC) and Accommodations Review Board (ARB) to maintain continuity and collaborate with other groups across campus.
- Maintains connection with the SJSU bookstore and SJSU librarians to provide accessible and inclusive access to course materials.
- Promotes events such as Global Accessibility Awareness Day and Fix Your Content Day.
- Communicates training opportunities related to accessibility and current accessibility tools. Examples: Meeting Your Students Where they’re At: Creating Inclusive Course Materials, SJSU Official Syllabus workshops and Audit Your Syllabus and Accessible Canvas Courses a self-paced courses.
