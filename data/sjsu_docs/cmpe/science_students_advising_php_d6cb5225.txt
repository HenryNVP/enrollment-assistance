Advising
Academic major and general education advising is provided by the College of Science Student Success center with information located on their Advising page. College of Science faculty in the respective departments are available for mentorship and research inquiries as well as for recommendations on upper division elective selection when appropriate.
Change of Major Advising
Current College of Science students or students declared in majors outside of the College of Science who are interested in changing majors to a College of Science major, can visit the College of Science Student Success Center for information on the change of major process, requirements, and deadlines.
