Welcome to the GBSA Mentorship Program!
The Graduate Business Student Association (GBSA) Mentorship Program is an innovative initiative designed to enrich the professional and personal development of both graduate and undergraduate business students. Through our near-peer mentoring approach, graduate students are paired with undergraduates, fostering growth and leadership across multiple domains. Participants engage in 4-6 scheduled meetings throughout the semester designed to cultivate mentoring mindsets, boost motivation to lead, and enhance leader self-efficacy.

Join as a Mentor
Becoming a GBSA Mentor is a unique opportunity to guide an undergraduate student through their academic and professional journey while earning valuable leadership experience. As a mentor, you will meet your mentee 4-6 times per semester, providing insights into navigating both education and career paths.
Program Benefits:
Potential to earn up to 7.5 SVE points.
Essential training and weekly mentoring tips.
Mentoring with GBSA is not only about enhancing someone else’s career but also about advancing your own.
Previous mentors in our program have reported significant personal and professional growth, making this a fulfilling experience.
*No prior mentoring experience required.
Join as a Mentee
Embark on your professional development journey with the GBSA Mentorship Program. As a mentee, you will gain critical skills that are essential in today’s business world, including networking, resume building, crafting an elevator pitch, and mastering interviews.
Participating in this program connects you with experienced graduate students who can provide valuable guidance and support as you navigate your educational and career aspirations.
