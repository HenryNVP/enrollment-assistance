Child Welfare and Attendance (CWA) Specialization
A Child Welfare and Attendance (CWA) specialization is a supplemental authorization that can be completed by individuals who are satisfying the requirements for the Pupil Personnel Services (PPS) K-12 School Counseling Credential.
The program of study for this optional authorization aligns with the preparation for our K-12 school counseling specialization. In order to meet the preparation standards outlined by the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing, relevant content about issues connected to child welfare and attendance is embedded into all of our school counseling specialization, practicum, and fieldwork courses. Students who decide to pursue this authorization will need to dedicate a minimum of 150 hours of supervised fieldwork to child welfare and attendance. This includes a minimum of 90 hours of fieldwork providing direct counseling to students in a school setting and a minimum of 30 hours in interdisciplinary experiences outside of the field of education.
Additional information about this authorization, program standards, and performance expectations can be found in the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing:
Pupil Personnel Services: Child Welfare and Attendance Added Authorization Preconditions, Program Standards, and Performance Expectations (September 2024)
