Permit News
- Most 2025 Annual, Academic, and Fall Semester parking permits are available for purchase
starting July 15, 2025, through the Parking Online Services website.
Please note: The South Campus fall semester virtual permits are valid at the new South Campus Garage (located at 10th and Alma Streets) and Park & Ride Lot. The South Campus fall semester virtual permits are also valid in general spaces of the main campus parking garages from 4:30 p.m. - 11:59 p.m. Monday - Thursday, and 7 a.m. - 11:59 p.m. Friday through Sunday. The South Campus permits are not valid for overnight parking. For additional permit information, visit Parking Permit page. - Starting June 1, 2022, only Semester and Academic overnight permits will be offered and available to students living on campus. Please plan accordingly.
