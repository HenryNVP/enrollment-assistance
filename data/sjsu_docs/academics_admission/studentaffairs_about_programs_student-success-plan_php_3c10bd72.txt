Student Success Plan
As the oldest public institution in California, San Jose State University has a long and proud history as a supplier of excellent higher education, a contributor to the skilled workforce in the Bay Area and an incubator for innovations that have significant local and global impact.
Through the decades, our priority has remained the same – to offer an exceptional and affordable education to all of our students so they find success in their careers and become engaged citizens of California.
