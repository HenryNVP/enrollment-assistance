How do I register for classes?
Current SJSU Students
Current SJSU students should review the steps to complete prior to registration, registration
basics, MySJSU tutorial, and plan your future terms.
Non-SJSU Students
Former SJSU students, current students from other universities, or community members,
can register for classes year-round through Open University.
