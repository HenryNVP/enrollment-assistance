Contact Us and Directions
The SpartUp Incubator was born during the COVID-19 pandemic, so we understand how important it is to support remote work, remote startups and remote events.
We are a remote-first incubator, utilizing collaboration and event space in the Martin Luther King, Jr. Library, located at 150 E. San Fernando Street on the SJSU campus. If you are attending an event at the MLK Library space, parking is available in the 4th and San Carlos Garage across the street.
If you are an SJSU student, alumni, faculty or staff and would like to join the incubator, please complete the membership application form.
If you are interested in partnering, corporate sponsorship, or have questions, please contact us at officeofinnovation@sjsu.edu.
