Title: Centers of Excellence
Type: HTML

The Department of Accounting and Finance teams with business professionals to expand
student opportunities. The Accounting Advancement Center, the Center for Banking
and Financial Services, and the High Technology Tax Institute combine with faculty
to deliver cutting-edge informational seminars, introduce students to industries,
and network with professionals in accounting, finance, and taxation.
The AAC connects accounting students and faculty to accounting firms in the Bay Area.
You can learn more about the AAC and its members on our dedicated page.
The CBFS connects finance students and faculty to banking and investment firms in
the Bay Area. You can learn more about the CBFS and its members on our dedicated page.
The HTI connects accounting and tax students and faculty to firms in the Bay Area.
You can learn more about the HTI and its members on our dedicated page.
