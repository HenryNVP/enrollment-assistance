Volunteer Opportunities
Volunteer at the Spartan Food Pantry
Join our mission to support fellow Spartans! From stocking shelves to assisting visitors, your time and energy help us create a welcoming and efficient environment for every student who walks through our doors.
Sign up today to gain valuable community service experience while providing essential resources to students in need!
Additional Information
Completing Community Service Learning Hours?
The authorized supervisor to approve and sign service learning hours is the Pantry's Basic Needs Coordinator.
Email spartanfoodpantry@sjsu.edu or brenjielyn.cuneta@sjsu.edu to send the file digitally or to find time to get it signed in-person at the SJSU Cares office.
