Group Campus Tour
Discover all that San Jose State University has to offer with our School/Group Tour program, designed for educational groups (up to 45 individuals per group). Led by experienced student ambassadors, the outdoor walking tour offers an in-depth look at campus life, academic programs, and our state-of-the-art facilities, with plenty of opportunities to ask questions and explore. | |
