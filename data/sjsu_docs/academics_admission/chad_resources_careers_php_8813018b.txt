Careers in Child and Adolescent Development
There are several resources at SJSU to help you explore career options related to child and adolescent development:
- Check out job postings on the bulletin board right outside of the ChAD office, which is located at Sweeney Hall (SH) 201.
- Browse through this Employment and Internship Opportunities Google Drive folder to find opportunities that may suit your interests and skills. Please pay close attention to the deadlines and requirements.
- The Student Success Center in SH 106 hosts several workshops and events throughhout the year. Stop by the center or check out their calendar of events on their website.
- The SJSU Career Center can help you with job application materials, interviewing strategies, and much more.
