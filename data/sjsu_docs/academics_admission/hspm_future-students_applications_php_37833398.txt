Tracking Application Status
You will receive a UserID and password after submitting your online application. Use this information to check the status of your application. You will be notified when you can register for classes once your application is successfully accepted by SJSU.
For additional information on Admissions Requirements check SJSU Admissions section of SJSU web site.
