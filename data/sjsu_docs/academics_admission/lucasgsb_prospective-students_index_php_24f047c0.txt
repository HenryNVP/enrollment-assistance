Prospective Lucas Graduate Business Students
As a student at the Lucas Graduate School of Business, you’re destined to join successful alumni holding top positions throughout Silicon Valley and in a variety of global and Fortune 500 companies and government agencies—Adobe, Amazon, Apple, Cisco, EY, Google, ServiceNow, Stanford, Tesla, and ServiceNow, for example.
Or perhaps you’ll join other alumni as startup entrepreneurs, angel investors and venture capitalists. Whatever your path, a Lucas School graduate degree will open doors and opportunities. There is no doubt that, equipped with what you will learn here, you’ll make a positive difference in Silicon Valley and the world.
To contact the Lucas Graduate School of Business with general questions, to learn more about our programs, or admission requirements, email: lucas-school@sjsu.edu or complete this survey.
