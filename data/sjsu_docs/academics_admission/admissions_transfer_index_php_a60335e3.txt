Title: Transfer Admissions
Type: HTML

We are honored that you are considering San Jose State University to further your
education. An upper division transfer applicant is a student that will have completed
at least 60 transferable semester units or 90 quarter units (and does not have a bachelor’s
degree). If you want to meet with a pre-admission counselor or take a live virtual
tour, visit our Student Outreach and Recruitment office at sjsu.edu/SOAR.
Check application and document deadlines, as well as deadlines for completing academic
requirements, applying for financial aid, and submitting a housing application to
live on campus.
