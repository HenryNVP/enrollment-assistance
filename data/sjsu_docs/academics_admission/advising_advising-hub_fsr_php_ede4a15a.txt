FSR - Former Student Returning
A former student returning (FSR) refers to a previously enrolled and fully admitted, degree-seeking student who has not attended SJSU as a regular student for the past two consecutive semesters and was not granted a leave of absence.
If you are ready to return, we are here to help!
Need help? Please visit the webpage for the Student Success Center that serves your major.
