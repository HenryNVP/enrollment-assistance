Women in Leadership League
2026 WILL Conference: Sponsored by Cisco
The 9th Annual Women in Leadership League Conference, proudly sponsored by Cisco, will take place on Friday, April 17, 2026. Led and hosted by Associate Dean of Faculty and Accreditation, Meghna Virick, the day is a mix of an inspirational fireside chat and breakout group discussions. Students, alumni, and mentors from all disciplines come together. Attendees will learn about career and life journeys from top Silicon Valley leaders.
Date: April 17, 2026
Time: 8:15am - 12:45pm
Location: Student Union Ballroom A and B
Host: Associate Dean Meghna Virick
Sponsor: Cisco

Program
8:15 - 9:00am – Check-in and Breakfast
Part A
9:00 - 10:00am – Leadership Across Generations Panel
Join Madison Huang, Senior Director of Product and Technical Marketing for Physical AI Platforms at NVIDIA; Lavanya Gopalakrishnan, Senior Vice President of Customer Success at Cisco; and Dr. Cynthia Teniente-Matson, President of San José State University, for a dynamic conversation on leadership across generations, moderated by Dean Rangapriya "Priya" Kannan. Representing artificial intelligence, global technology, and higher education, these leaders will share the pivotal moments that shaped their careers, the lessons that influenced their leadership, and their perspective on the future of leadership in a rapidly evolving world.
Part B
Networking with a Purpose: Birds of a Feather table discussions with Industry Mentors from top Silicon Valley Companies.

- Session 3: 11:55 - 12:25pm
