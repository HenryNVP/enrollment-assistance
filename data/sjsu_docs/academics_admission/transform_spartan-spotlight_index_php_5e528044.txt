The Muwekma Ohlone Are Still Here
Thousands of years before SJSU existed, the Muwekma Ohlone Tribe thrived along the
waterway now known as the Guadalupe River. This summer, the arts collective Empire
Seven Studios unveiled “We Are Muwekma Ohlone,” a mural by Alfonso Salazar that honors
the past, present and future of the tribe.
