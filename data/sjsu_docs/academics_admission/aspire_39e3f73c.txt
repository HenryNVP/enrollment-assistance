Since 1977, the TRIO (SSS/ASPIRE) Program has been helping first-generation, low-income,
and students with disabilities with class scheduling, major/career exploration, academic
advising and financial aid assistance. We support and motivate our students towards
a successful completion of their college education.
The Federal TRIO Programs aim to identify and provide services for individuals from
disadvantaged backgrounds. This includes individuals who are low-income, first-generation
college students, and individuals with disabilities.
