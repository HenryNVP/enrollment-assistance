CEGO (Counselor Education Graduate Organization)
CEGO creates an inclusive community for graduate students studying in the Counselor Education Department of the Connie L. Lurie College of Education by providing networking opportunities, practical experiences, and a space to voice opinions. Connect with CEGO on Instagram @cegostudentorg.
