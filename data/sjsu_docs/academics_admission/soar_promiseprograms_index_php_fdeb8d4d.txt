SJSU Promise Programs
San José State University offers Promise Programs that create clear pathways to college and support student success from high school through graduation. These initiatives are designed to expand access, simplify the admission process, and provide guidance to students and their families. Through partnerships with local school districts, SJSU Promise Programs help ensure that more students have the opportunity to pursue higher education and achieve their academic goals.
