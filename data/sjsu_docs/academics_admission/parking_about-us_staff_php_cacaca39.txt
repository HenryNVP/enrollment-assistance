Parking Services Staff
Parking Services is composed of administration, enforcement, maintenance and shuttle program units:
Parking Administration Unit
Six full-time staff and several student assistants provide customer service in the sales and distribution of parking permits and the administration of the parking citation program.
Parking Enforcement Unit
Four full-time parking officers and two student assistants patrol the parking facilities providing both security service and enforcement of the rules and regulations. See Parking Maps.
Parking Maintenance Unit
One full-time staff maintains parking equipment and pay stations. We also have an in-house sign shop and produce most of the signage displayed in our facilities.
Parking Shuttle Program
Seven fully trained bus drivers provide complimentary service in our fleet of Spartan Express buses to and from the Park & Ride Lot.
