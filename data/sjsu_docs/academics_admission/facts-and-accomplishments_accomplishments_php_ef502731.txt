CSU Economic Impact Report 2025
SJSU is recognized as the #1 Regional CSU for Economic Impact.
Out of CSUs with enrollment above 30k, SJSU leads its region with the highest shares
of jobs created in the private sector, contributions to the respective region’s labor
incomes, and the respective region’s economy-wide business output.
Spartans also make a statewide impact:
We contribute $2 billion in annual business output across California and are the #4
CSU campus for the total additional income its alumni earn as a result of their degrees.
Learn more about the SJSU impact.
U.S. Department of Education
2025 Postsecondary Success Recognition Program Winner
The U.S. Department of Education announced SJSU as one of six winners of the first-ever Postsecondary Success Recognition Program (PSRP). Launched in April 2024 by U.S. Secretary of Education Miguel Cardona, the program celebrates institutions of higher education that are enrolling underserved student populations, facilitating successful student transfers and completions, and equipping graduates for careers that lead to economic mobility.
