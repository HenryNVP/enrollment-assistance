Academics
The Department of Counselor Education prepares students to serve as professional practitioners in guidance, counseling, and student personnel work. The graduate program encompasses both a Master of Arts (MA) degree and Pupil Personnel Services (PPS) K-12 School Counseling Credential. The department also offers undergraduate courses to support students in their personal, academic, and career exploration.
