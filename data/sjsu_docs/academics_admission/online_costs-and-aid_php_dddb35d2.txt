Application Fee
The cost to submit your application through the Cal State Apply portal is $70. When you apply for admission, you are automatically considered for an application fee waiver based on the information requested in the application.
Oregon Tuition Refund Policy
According to the Oregon Administrative Rules (OAR 583-030-0035(18)(c)), after classes begin for a term, an Oregon student who withdraws from a course is eligible for a partial refund through the middle week of the term. Refunds shall be based on unused instructional time and shall be prorated on a weekly basis for schools using a semester calendar.
Numerous Financial Aid Opportunities
- Federal Financial Aid: You are eligible to apply for Federal Student Aid and to qualify for Federal Pell Grants. Complete the Free Application for Federal Financial Aid (FAFSA) application as soon as you can to provide enough time for processing prior to your first term.
-
Federal Work-Study Positions: Students who receive Federal Work-Study can apply for eligible positions through the SJSU Handshake portal, which hosts job and internship opportunities for students and alumni.
- Cal Grants: Students who complete the FAFSA application and meet certain eligibility requirements can also apply for various California Student Aid Commission grant awards. If you are awarded a Cal Grant, you must be enrolled in at least 6 units in order to receive it.
- SJSU Scholarships: Each spring, students can apply online for university-wide and college scholarships through the Financial Aid and Scholarship Office for the upcoming academic year.
- SJSU Veterans Resource Center: If you are a military-connected individual, please contact the SJSU Veterans Resource Center to discuss your benefits and eligibility for special session programs.
-
SJSU Online Student Ambassadors: Current SJSU Online students can apply for paid student ambassador positions each term to provide current and potential students with valuable insights, resources, and experiences.
-
Amazon Career Choice: Through our partnership, current Amazon employees can visit the Amazon Career Choice website for details about how to utilize that benefit to apply for enrollment at SJSU.
