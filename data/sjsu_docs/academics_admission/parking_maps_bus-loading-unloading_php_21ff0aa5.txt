Bus Loading/Unloading Areas
Buses needing to drop-off passengers, please utilize our bus loading/unloading areas located at Duncan Hall (San Salvador St. & 5th St.) or at the Student Service Center (9th St. & San Fernando St.). See map below. Please do not use the red zone areas in front of the South Garage or by the SRAC on 7th Street for bus loading/unloading. Violators will be cited.
If you need to park your bus(es) while you wait to pick up your passenger, you may use the bus staging area at the Park & Ride Lot (S. 7th St. & Humboldt St.). See map below. Please note that a valid parking permit is required for each space the bus is parked in. Parking permit may be purchased via Parkmobile app or Pay Station located at the Park & Ride Lot.
