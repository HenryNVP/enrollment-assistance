Get Involved
COACHE at SJSU is an ongoing process! Faculty engagement does not end when the survey closes. Participation in discussions, governance processes, and working groups helps us ensure that findings are interpreted thoughtfully and acted on collaboratively.
Questions or suggestions about COACHE? Reach out at any time to faculty-success@sjsu.edu and/or senate@sjsu.edu.
