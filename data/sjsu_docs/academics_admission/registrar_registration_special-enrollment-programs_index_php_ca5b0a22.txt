Over-Sixty Program (Discontinued):
Due to severe budget cuts and impaction, San Jose State University can no longer offer the Over Sixty Program. This program has been discontinued as of Fall 2012.
You may visit CSU campuses website to see whether any other campuses are still offering this program.
