Parent and Family Programs
Parents and families, welcome to the Spartan family! Congratulations on your student’s enrollment in San Jose State. Your role in your student’s life now is as important as ever. We want to support you as you support your student. The best way to do that is to stay connected: to them and to SJSU.
Parent and Family Freshman Orientation
Join us during Frosh Orientation to learn more about SJSU and how you can play a supportive role in helping your student succeed.
