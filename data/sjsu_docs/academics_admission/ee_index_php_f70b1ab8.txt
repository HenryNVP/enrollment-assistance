Spring 2026 Student Project Symposium
Spring 2026 Student Project Symposium will be on Monday, May 11, 2026 from 9:00 AM to 12(noon) at SJSU Student Union Ballroom. More than 50 senior and master projects in many EE areas, including embedded systems, machine learning, computer vision, robotics, communications, IoT, nano electronics, power and energy systems, quantum information and computing, etc. will be presented at the event. The event is open to the general public.
