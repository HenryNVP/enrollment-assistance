Giving to the School of Management
The School of Management is honored to receive contributions that support our mission. Our fundraising initiatives aim to cultivate partnerships with donors, enhancing our mission and extending our impact on our students, alumni, and community partners.
We invite donors to contribute to the School of Management’s Scholarship and Research Support fund. Donations of any amount are greatly appreciated and will directly benefit our students and research initiatives.
Contributions can be made online via credit/debit card or by mail via check.
For checks, please make payable to "Tower Foundation", and send to:
School of Management - BT 650
San Jose State University
129 S. 10th Streeet
San Jose, CA 95192-1244.
For those interested in discussing their gift or specifying its use, please reach out to Dr. Susanna Khavul at susanna.khavul@sjsu.edu.
Our objective is to ensure all students receive a comprehensive education by developing the quantitative, critical thinking, and scientific skills necessary for lifelong learning and informed decision-making on scientific issues. We are deeply grateful to our loyal supporters who have helped us make significant strides towards this goal.
