Student Success Centers
We understand that there are many factors in the road to success. The Undergraduate Advising Hub and Student Success centers are vital in helping students thrive, academically, personally and professionally. Their primary role is to help students stay enrolled and keep moving forward in their education while making progress towards a degree. In other words, we want students to stay enrolled and stick with their goal to graduate. Both are important to student success, and we are here to help students every step of the way!
