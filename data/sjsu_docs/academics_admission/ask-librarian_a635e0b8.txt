Title: Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Library: Ask Us: Get Help
Type: HTML

Did you know that there's a librarian for every subject taught at SJSU? Contact your subject librarian any time you need help with your research.
You can also use Live Chat during our reference hours to get help, or use the E-mail form below to submit any questions you have.
E-mail Us
Please submit your question below. You can usually expect a response within 24 hours from Monday through Friday when classes are in session. You can also search our collection of Frequently Asked Questions.
