Title: Parking Services Highlights
Type: HTML

The South Campus Park & Ride Lot is half price at $4 a day (or $96 per semester).
The single-day maximum rate for evenings (after 5:30 p.m.) and weekends has been reduced
to $5
Pay-Stations
Automated Permit Pay-Stations are available in all 3 garages and Lot 4.
Pay-Stations accept coins, $1, $5, $10 bills and Visa/MasterCard.
Transportation Programs
Parking Services provides funding to help subsidize:
Vanpool program
DASH Shuttle
Highway 17 Express monthly passes
VTA EcoPasses
Complimentary Parking
Complimentary parking is provided at the South Campus Park & Ride Lot during the first week of each semester to reduce traffic congestion at the main campus.
Traffic Congestion Relief
SJSU employees and on-campus residents will always find parking available in the South
Garage — a portion of the roof is reserved for their use.The South Garage exit ramp
is reversed from 7:15 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. during the first 2 weeks of each semester
to allow traffic to enter from E. San Salvador Street, reducing congestion on S. 7th
Street.
