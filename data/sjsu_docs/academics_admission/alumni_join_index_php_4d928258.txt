Title: Join the Alumni Association
Type: HTML

Joining the Alumni Association is one of the best ways to show your Spartan Pride.
Alumni membership connects you to a network of graduates throughout the country who
represent the ideals of San José State University. Strengthen the value of your degree,
access career resources, enjoy money-saving benefits, and stay connected to your alma
mater that is powering the world. Please note, lifetime membership is non-refundable.
Thank you for showing your Spartan pride and investing in your alma mater!
