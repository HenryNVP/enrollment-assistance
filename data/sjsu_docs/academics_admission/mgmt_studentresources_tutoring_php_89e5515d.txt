Tutoring Resources
College-Based
Jack Holland Student Success Center
Locations: Bocardo Business Complex 303 and Business Tower 053
We offer drop-in and appointment-based tutoring for a variety of specific Business courses.
Writing, and Communication
The Writing Center
Location: 2nd Floor of King Library
We offer drop-in and appointment-based tutoring for all types of writing to all students. We also offer numerous workshops each semester in addition to online resources.
Communication Center
Location: Hugh Gillis Hall 229
We offer drop-in and appointment-based tutoring for speeches, essays, and presentations to all students.
Multiple-Subjects
Peer Connections
Location: Student Services Center 600
We offer appointment-based one-on-one and small group tutoring for general writing and for over 130 courses from a variety of departments.
Technology
Student Technology Training Center
Location: King Library, 4th floor
We offer software training classes, such as SPSS and MS Office, and drop-in technology support.
