Governance & Public Documents for SJSURF
The San José State University Research Foundation provides an entrepreneurial framework through which local, state and federal agencies, businesses and private foundations engage SJSU faculty and students in sponsored research, public service and community projects, consulting and other specialized educational activities in support of the university’s mission by:
- supporting faculty success,
- expanding student horizons,
- delivering specialized business services, and
- developing partnerships with the community.
Learn more about how we foster the university's quest for excellence in our complete mission statement [pdf].
