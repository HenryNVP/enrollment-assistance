Prospective MST Students
Applications for admission to the MST program are not currently being accepted for the Academic Year 2025 – 2026. Please consider our 9-unit Tax Practice Certificate, MSAA, MSFA, and MBA programs.
Our 9-unit Certificate in Tax Practice includes a 3-unit course on tax research to enable you to find, understand, apply and explain tax laws, plus courses covering taxation of different types of business entities and transactions.
