Student Work Opportunities
The School of Management offers our students the unique opportunity to gain valuable, real-world experience related to their field of study through part-time employment while they are enrolled in our programs.
Each semester, we hire student assistants based on the needs of our college and available funding. The roles of these assistants vary, providing support in areas such as course management, research, and administrative tasks.
For additional job opportunities, both on and off-campus, we recommend students to utilize SJSU Handshake, our primary resource for employment.
We also encourage students to explore employment opportunities with other on-campus employers, including:
- Associated Students
- Martin Luther King Jr. Library
- Orientation Leaders
- Peer Connections
- Police Department
- Resident Advisors/Housing
- Spartan Eats
- Student Union
- Writing Center
We look forward to your contributions to our vibrant and diverse community. Welcome to the Spartan family!
