Title: International Transfer
Type: HTML

We are honored that you are considering San Jose State University to further your
education. As an international transfer applicant, you are a student who is a citizen
of another country who would like to earn your first bachelor's degree, have attended
another college or university, and require an F-1 visa or J-1 visa to study in the
U.S. If you want to meet with a pre-admission counselor or take a live virtual tour,
visit our Student Outreach and Recruitment office at sjsu.edu/SOAR.
Check application and document deadlines, as well as deadlines for completing academic
requirements and submitting a housing application to live on campus.
