Research Development
Our mission is to maximize the institutional capacity for extramural funding by building a robust SJSU research culture in support of faculty-led, student-focused, multidisciplinary research that empowers SJSU to lead in the creation of public impact research delivering social and economic benefits to our local, regional, and global communities.
Tell Us About Your Research!
If you have a RSCA accomplishment, we want to know about it! We'd love to hear about your recent achievement and potentially share it out with others on social media or with the SJSU NewsCenter. Share information about your RSCA accomplishments here.
