Distinguished Alumni Awards - March 12, 2026
Thank You for Joining Us at Spartans of Significance 2026 On behalf of the SJSU Alumni Association, thank you to everyone who joined us on March 12 to celebrate our remarkable 2026 honorees. Your presence made the evening truly special. A heartfelt thank you to our generous sponsors whose support helps fund the Alumni Association Scholarship Program — and to our guests and volunteers who brought the night to life. We look forward to celebrating with you again next year. Go Spartans!

The Spartans of Significance: Distinguished Alumni Awards celebrate exceptional Spartans who have made a profound impact in their fields and uplifted the San José State University community. This prestigious program honors alumni for remarkable achievements, including professional excellence, dedicated community service, and a commitment to SJSU’s values. Through the Spartans of Significance, we proudly showcase the diverse accomplishments of SJSU’s community, inspiring pride and connection among alumni, students, and friends of the university. This event goes beyond celebration—proceeds directly support the SJSU Alumni Association Scholarship Program, helping students achieve their dreams.
