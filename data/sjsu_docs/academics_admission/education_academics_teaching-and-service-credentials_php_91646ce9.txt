Connie L. Lurie College of Education
Multiple Subject Teaching Credential (w/Bilingual Authorization Option)
Credential for individuals interested in teaching multiple subjects (K-8) with an optional Bilingual Authorization in Elementary Education
Single Subject Teaching Credential (w/Bilingual Authorization Option)
Prepares invididuals to serve in dual immersion or bilingual school settings in California for Secondary Education
Early Childhood Special Education
Credential program that prepares fully qualified teachers to work with families and their young children with disabilities or developmental delays
Mild-Moderate Support Needs Education Specialist
This program prepares individuals to support kindergarten through age 22 who have mild to moderate disabilities
Extensive Needs Support Education Specialist
Prepares individuals to teach students from kindergarten through age 22 who have disabilities requiring extensive support
Pupil Personnel Services School Counseling
Program for individuals interested in becoming a credentialed school counselor in a public elementary, middle, or high school setting in California
PK-3 Early Childhood Education Specialist
Prepare individuals who are interested in teaching pre-kindergarten through third grade
PK-3 Interest Form
Speech Language Pathology
Service credential for individuals interested in becoming a speech-lanuguage pathologist in a public school setting
Early Childhood and Transitional Kindergarten Teaching
This program is for Multiple Subject credential holders who need to complete coursework to meet the requirements for teaching transitional kindergarten
Adapted Physical Education
Credential program offered by the Department of Kinesiology
PPS Social Work with Child Welfare Attendance
Credential program offered by the School of Social Work
Math Integrated Teacher Prep Program
Credential program offered by the Department of Mathematics
