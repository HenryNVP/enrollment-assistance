Mark and Kathy Loveless: Rewarding Spartan Service
Before becoming a Spartan, Mark Loveless, ’78 Accounting, served in the military. A career accountant, Mark dedicated 35 years to serving Bay Area clients while his wife Kathy worked in healthcare. After retiring, they established the Mark and Kathy Loveless Scholarship for service members and military veterans in accounting.
“We want to aid those who have served our country and need help continuing their studies so they can be productive citizens,” says Kathy.
The couple made provisions in their estate plan to permanently endow the fund. Veterans and service members will benefit from their generosity for years to come.
You can join Mark and Kathy and help San Jose State's Lucas College and Graduate School of Business prepare the next generation of business professionals with a gift in your will or a gift that pays you income. Learn more at Legacy.sjsu.edu.
