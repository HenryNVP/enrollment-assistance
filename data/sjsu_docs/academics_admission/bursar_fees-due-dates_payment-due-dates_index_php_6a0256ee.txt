Payment Due Dates
Students are responsible for keeping track of payment dates. Conveniently, payment due dates are posted in the finances section of your MySJSU account immediatelly after you register for classes. For step-by-step instructions, see How to View Payment Due Dates [pdf].
Payment due dates also posted online and updated right before each semester's registration period. Select the appropriate semester below:
Open University Students
Open University payment due dates are different. Visit the Open University calendar for payment due dates or email Professional and Continuing Education (PaCE) at pace@sjsu.edu.
Special Session Students
Special Session payment due dates depend on your enrollment date. Check your due dates in MySJSU or email Professional and Continuing Education (PaCE) at pace@sjsu.edu.
