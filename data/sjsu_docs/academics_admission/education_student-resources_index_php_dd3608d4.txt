Student Resources for Success
The Success Center at Lurie College
We believe that academic advising is a collaborative and transformative educational process where students and their advisors work jointly to develop plans for achievement of the students’ personal, academic, and professional goals. Our Lurie College of Education Student Success Center provides academic advising about General Education (GE) requirements, changing a major/minor, and support for students on academic probation or disqualified from the university. Our Lurie College undergraduate, graduate, credential, and doctoral students can also utilize our wide range of resources.

Commission on Teacher Credentialing (CTC) Resources
With the new Roadmap to Teaching, you can explore differences in experience, time commitment, advantages and considerations, and financial support available for each of four major teacher preparation pathways.
Aspiring Educators Resources
Additionally, the CTC offers financial resources such as:
Funding Your Future in Teaching [pdf] offers financial planning steps for every aspiring educator and sample funding packages
for those considering a California-based teacher residency program.
Teacher Candidate Funding Explorer is a searchable database of federal and state dollars teacher candidates may be eligible to collect to make their journey toward a teaching credential affordable or cost-free.
Teacher Preparation Program Leader Resources
Teacher Residency Program Funding Scenarios identify realistic strategies for combining multiple funding streams to provide robust, durable support to develop and sustain a teacher preparation program of this type.
Teacher Program Funding Explorer is a searchable database of federal and state dollars they may apply to make their teacher preparation programs more attractive and valuable.
