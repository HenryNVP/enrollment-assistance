MA, Teaching
If you completed our SJSU Single Subject or Multiple Subject Credential Program within the last 6 years, have received your Preliminary Credential from CTC, and want to add a Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT), then we invite you to apply to our new MAT program.
Note:
- Coursework is valid for 7 years, if your coursework is older than 7 years, you may have to repeat courses.
- ITEP program completers are not eligible. Consider instead the MA Multilingual and Multicultural Literacy Education program.
Application Requirements

- Submit transcripts for your undergraduate and credential coursework. Unofficial transcripts

- Statement of Purpose
-
In a maximum of 500 words, address the following in your statement of purpose:
-
How does your interest in returning to complete the MAT degree align with the Lurie College of Education Strategic Plan and the Teacher Education Department Vision and Mission [pdf]?
-
How will your completion of the MAT program help you to serve your communities (classroom, school, and/or beyond)?

- Submit a brief letter from your school’s principal on school letterhead.
- Submit a brief letter from your school’s principal on school letterhead.
- Letter of Recommendation
-
Submit one (1) letter of recommendation, addressed to the Department of Teacher Education. A letter of recommendation submitted as part of a job application is not appropriate for the MAT application. The letter must:
-
Be specific to returning for the Master’s in Teaching (MAT)
-
Indicate specific qualifications for a graduate program
-
Indicate potential to contribute in the future
-
Be from people in a supervisory role who have known you in a professional or educational setting
-
Speak to your ability to work with people, experience with youth, your work ethic, your intellectual capabilities, and / or other characteristics pertinent to teaching
