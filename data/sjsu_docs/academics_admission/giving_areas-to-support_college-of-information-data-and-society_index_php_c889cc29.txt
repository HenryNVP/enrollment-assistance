Give to College of Information, Data and Society
Support the Future of Information, Data and Society at SJSU
At the College of Information, Data and Society, we are committed to shaping the future by investing in the brightest minds and advancing cutting-edge fields such as data analysis, artificial intelligence and information science. Your generous contributions can help us achieve these goals and ensure our students have the resources they need to succeed.
Advance the Field of Data Analysis, AI and Information Science
In an era where data drives decision-making, the fields of data analysis, artificial intelligence and information science are more critical than ever. Your donations help fund groundbreaking research, foster industry partnerships and support the development of innovative technologies that can transform industries and society.
Invest in Innovation for Future Leaders
In today’s fast-paced world, innovation in data and information sciences is paramount. Investing in these areas equips future leaders and organizations with the tools and knowledge to respond effectively to the evolving needs of the communities they serve. Your support enables us to offer cutting-edge approaches that address the dynamic data and information challenges of the future. By fostering innovation, we prepare our students to become pioneers who drive progress and positive change across various sectors.
The Power of Alumni Support
Our alumni are our greatest ambassadors and supporters. Your success and achievements are a testament to the quality of education and experiences provided by SJSU. By giving back, you help pave the way for future generations of students to follow in your footsteps and make their mark in the world.
Your gifts can:
- Provide scholarships to deserving students, helping them to achieve their academic and career goals.
- Fund research projects that push the boundaries of knowledge and innovation.
- Support the development of new programs and initiatives that keep our curriculum relevant
and cutting-edge.
How You Can Make a Difference
- One-Time Donation: Every contribution, no matter the size, helps us make a significant impact. A one-time donation can support various initiatives such as purchasing new software, updating lab equipment or funding student projects.
- Recurring Donation: Consistent support allows us to plan for the future and ensure continuous improvement in our programs. By setting up a recurring donation, you provide a steady stream of resources that enable long-term projects and sustained student support.
- Endowment Funds: Establishing or contributing to an endowment fund creates a lasting legacy. Endowment funds generate annual income that supports scholarships, faculty research and program development in perpetuity.
