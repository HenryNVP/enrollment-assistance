SAGE, Child and Adolescent Development
Our Spartan Accelerated Graduate Education (SAGE) Programs are combined bachelor’s and master’s programs at SJSU. In some cases, they may also satisfy the requirements for a credential. Students in these programs pursue both degrees simultaneously, allowing them to earn graduate credit during their junior and/or senior year. This structure reduces the number of semesters required to complete a master’s degree.

Initial Eligibility and Requirements
For details on initial eligibility, please visit the SJSU SAGE Overview webpage. In addition, students must also meet the requirements for the ChAD SAGE programs:
- 3.0 GPA at the time of transition into the graduate or credential program
- Completion of at least 60 units but not more than 80 units
- Successful completion of the GWAR requirement (ChAD 100W)
All prospective SAGE students must meet with a SAGE coordinator to confirm eligibility. To schedule advising, please complete the ChAD SAGE Interest Form, and an advisor will reach out.

Steps to Apply
Current SJSU Students
Meet with a ChAD SAGE program coordinator by completing the ChAD SAGE Interest Form. Students are advised to apply to the ChAD SAGE program once they have successfully completed at least 60 units, but no more than 80 units.
Former SJSU ChAD Graduates
Please apply directly to the credential and/or master's programs.
