Welcome!
(Updated as of 5/23/23). The Timpany Center is a non-profit educational and therapeutic service center that serves community members through a partnership between San José State University Research Foundation (SJSURF) and Santa Clara County. COVID-19 in Santa Clara County remains at a “Low” community level as determined by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). In this context COVID-19 vaccinations are recommended as a safety measure to help prevent serious illness, but as of June 1st the Timpany Center will no longer require proof of vaccination to use the facility.
