About Us
Parking Services is dedicated to providing a clean and safe parking environment for students, faculty, staff and visitors.
We maintain 7,500 parking spaces for a campus population of over 32,000 people.
As part of the University Police Department, we provide the following services to the campus community:
- Courtesy Motorist Assistance
- Park & Ride Courtesy Shuttle
- Safety Escort to Your Car
- Parking Enforcement
- Special Event Parking
- Student, Employee & Visitor Parking Permits
If you have a question or need our help, contact us at 408-924-6557.
