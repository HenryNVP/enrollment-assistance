Individual and Family Campus TourDiscover San Jose State University through our one-hour Individual and Family Campus Tour. Led by our knowledgeable student ambassadors, this tour is perfect for prospective students and their families who want to get an inside look at our campus. During the tour, you'll explore our beautiful campus, visit our state-of-the-art facilities, and learn about our academic programs, student life, and support services. Our campus tour is a great way to experience the vibrant and diverse community that is San Jose State University. | |
