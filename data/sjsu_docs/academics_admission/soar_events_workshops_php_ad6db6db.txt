Next Steps Workshops
Informational sessions are for Fall 2026 admits only.
We're offering several virtual Next Steps workshops to help you complete your Intent to Enroll for Fall 2026. During these sessions, Admission Counselors will walk you through the final steps of the enrollment process and answer your questions live.
Each workshop includes:
- An overview of the required Next Steps process, including saying “Yes,” registering for orientation, and paying your enrollment deposit
- Information about student resources and services
- A live Q&A with our Admissions Counselors

Supplemental Application Workshop
Informational Session for Fall 2026 Upper Division Transfer applicants only
This session offered via Zoom will help you prepare for the mandatory SJSU Supplemental Application (Supp App). Not only will you learn what the steps are in the admissions process directly from our Admissions Counselors, but you will also learn what you need to do to finalize your application to SJSU. Join us for:
- A brief overview of the supplemental application and why it’s important
- Tips on updating your Spring 2026 college enrollment, as well as verifying your transferable units completed and planned through the end of Spring 2026
- Insight on how to input your major-specific preparatory coursework
- Tips on updating, deleting, or adding information for completed Associate Degrees for Transfer (ADT) from a community college
- A brief refresher on admissions requirements and upcoming important dates and deadlines for transfers
- A summary of exciting events and opportunities here at SJSU
- A live Q&A with our Student Outreach and Recruitment team for general questions
