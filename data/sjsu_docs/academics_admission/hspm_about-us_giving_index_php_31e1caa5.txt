Giving to HTEM
The Department of Hospitality, Tourism, and Event Management welcomes donations. If you would like to discuss a gift, please contact Dr. Tsu-Hong Yen at either (408) 924-3292 or Tsu-Hong.Yen@sjsu.edu to explain your preferences.
Currently, donors have the option to support the Department of HTEM through the Chair's Fund for Excellence and/or HTEM Undergraduate Scholarship Support by making a gift in the amount of their choosing. In this case, please make check payable to "Tower Foundation", and send to:
Department of HTEMSan Jose State University
One Washington Square
San Jose, CA 95192-0211.
In addition, for information on how you may leave a gift by credit card, contact Dr. Tsu-Hong Yen, tsu-hong.yen@sjsu.edu, (408) 924-3292.
