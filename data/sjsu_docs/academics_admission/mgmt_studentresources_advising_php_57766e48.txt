Academic Advising
The Jack Holland Student Success Center (JHSSC) is the central advisement office for all undergraduate business majors. They help with academic planning (includes general education and major course requirements), the graduation application process, understanding college and university policies and procedures, and advice for students on academic probation or those disqualified.
All advising requests, including change of major, are conducted by the Jack Holland Student Success Center.
The JHSSC is located in the Boccardo Business Complex (BBC 008), on the lower level. A Tower ID is required for entry.
Please visit their website to schedule an appointment or to join a drop-in session. You may also call +1-408-924-3435 for more information.
