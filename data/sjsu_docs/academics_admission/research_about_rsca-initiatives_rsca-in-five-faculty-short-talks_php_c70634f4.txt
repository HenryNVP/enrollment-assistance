Research & Innovation Exchange Series
The Research and Innovation Exchange Series (RIES) builds upon the success of RSCA
in 5 to offer a dynamic forum that highlights the breadth of research, scholarship,
and creative activity across campus. The series showcases faculty and student work
through datablitz presentations and focused discussion, creating space for meaningful
dialogue around emerging ideas and interdisciplinary connections. RIES also provides
opportunities to engage with the broader community and industry representatives to
support collaboration, knowledge exchange, and the amplification of SJSU’s research
impact.
In person and via livestream Friday, November 14, 2025 at the Martin Luther King Library,
Room 225. Talks are from 11 a.m. to 12 p.m., followed by in person lunch.
RSVP for the event by November 11, 2025
