SJSU Cares
No matter what students' needs are, we're here to support them. Our SJSU Cares team is ready and available to help any student with basic needs, as well as medical, emergency and clothing assistance. The Spartan Food Pantry offers fresh produce and other non-perishable goods to eligible students. We know that when students have their basic needs met, it enhances their well-being and student experience as a whole.
