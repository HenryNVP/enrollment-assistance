About Us
As the # 1 "Most Transformative College" in the nation, San José State is ready for big change. As part of Transformation 2030, SJSU's strategic plan, the campus community is dedicated to realizing the university's
potential as a nationally prominent public university.
The Office of Innovation is focused on growing industry collaboration, creating meaningful
impact from SJSU innovation, and growing the number and quality of SJSU-affiliated
startup companies. We work closely with the SJSU Research Foundation who provides administrative and business support to the innovation enterprise.
The Office of Innovation is part of the Division of Research and Innovation. Discover
the division's vision, mission and values.
