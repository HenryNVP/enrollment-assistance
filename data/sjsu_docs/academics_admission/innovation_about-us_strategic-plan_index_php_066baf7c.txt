Strategic Plan
Mission
Grow a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship by supporting students, faculty, and staff to develop and translate University creations and expand the impact of SJSU Research and Innovation.
Vision
To become a valued and sustainable resource for developing and translating SJSU innovation into products, services, and new companies that dive research and provide economic and social benefit.
Strategic Goals
- Enhance translation of SJSU innovation through effective intellectual property management services (Intellectual Property Management)
- Expand entrepreneurship culture and opportunities across SJSU that help SJSU-affiliated entrepreneurs start and build successful companies (SpartUp Incubator and Silicon Valley Small Business Development Center)
- Develop and grow relationships with industry for research & development programs (Industry Alliances)
- Create sustainable programmatic growth of OICP initiatives by leveraging grant funding and creating programs that support donor-based funding initiatives
- Develop work practices that address core customer needs and result in an outstanding customer service that meets or exceeds customer expectations
