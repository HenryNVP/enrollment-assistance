CalTPA Equipment Request
All teacher candidates are required by the State of California to pass an approved
teacher performance assessment prior to applying for their preliminary Multiple Subject,
Single Subject, or Education Specialist Credential.
SJSU Lurie College of Education has adopted the California Teacher Performance Assessment
(CalTPA) for our teacher credential programs. Within your coursework and student teaching
you will have many opportunities to engage in practice opportunities to prepare you
for the CalTPA, and more importantly, classroom teaching.
The Lurie College of Education wants to support you as you're completing your CalTPA requirements. If you need to borrow equipment to complete your assessment, please complete the Google form below so our IT team can follow up with you.
