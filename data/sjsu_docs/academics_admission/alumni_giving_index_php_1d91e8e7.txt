Giving
Donating to San Jose State is a meaningful way to give back and support the institution that played a significant role in your personal and educational growth. Your contributions help fund scholarships, enhance academic programs, and improve campus facilities, ultimately providing current and future students with opportunities and resources that can shape their futures and create a positive impact on society. By giving back to your alma mater, you are not only expressing gratitude but also investing in the continued success and growth of the institution that helped shape your own journey.
Visit the Giving website and explore more options to support specific colleges, societies and athletic teams at SJSU!
