Admitted Student Receptions and Events
Admitted Spartan Day 2026
Saturday, April 18, 2026
Congratulations on your provisional admission to San Jose State University for Fall 2026! We are excited to welcome you and your families to our Admitted Spartan Day (ASD).
This event is FREE and open only to newly admitted Fall 2026 students and their guests to experience what life is like at SJSU. Engage in special topic workshops, academic open houses, and activities such as a campus tour and resource fair as you prepare to become a Spartan!
More information and the program can be found at sjsu.edu/admittedspartanday
For event info, you can call us at 408-924-2564, or email admittedspartan@sjsu.edu
