To support and represent the students of San José State University by continuing the organization’s legacy of student advocacy and leadership; To enhance SJSU students’ education through high quality programs and services; and, to prepare students as they move towards a thoughtful and purposeful life after graduation.
Check our Jobs listings to find open positions at all of our departments. From student election officers and marketing coordinators to teacher assistants and computer repair specialists, we’re always looking for qualified students to join our team!
Associated Students provides funding to support student clubs, groups, and organizations across campus. Whether you want to hire a guest speaker, have t-shirts made, or pay for food at your next event, complete the online form to request funds.
