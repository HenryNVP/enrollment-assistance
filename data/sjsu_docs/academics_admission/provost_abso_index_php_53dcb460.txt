Academic Business and Strategic Operations (ABSO)
Academic Business and Strategic Operations (ABSO) is a unit dedicated to organizational success across the Division of Academic Affairs. Incorporating academic projects, customer service, change management and budget services, the goal of ABSO is to centralize support for our colleges and units to help address problems, assess changes, generate process improvements and create meaningful transformation through campus-wide collaboration.
