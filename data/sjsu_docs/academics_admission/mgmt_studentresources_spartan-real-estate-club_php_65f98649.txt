Spartan Real Estate Association (SREA)
The Spartan Real Estate Association (SREA) was founded as a student club in August 2024 to provide programs and benefits for San José State University Students to explore and ultimately secure meaningful professional careers and internships in the dynamic Real Estate Industry. Presently the Club is 70 members strong and growing.
Major Programs & Membership Benefits for SREA members:
- Industry Panels & Guest Speakers
- Alumni Mentorships
- Personal Career Guidance
- Internships and Career Placement
- Skill Development Courses
- Financial Scholarships & Grants
SREA members are exposed to diverse career opportunities in Real Estate including:
- Real Estate Appraisal
- Property Management
- Corporate Real Estate
- Commercial Leasing Brokerage
- Commercial Investment Sales
- Commercial Mortgage Brokerage
- Hotel Management
- Residential Sales Agency
- Real Estate Financial Analysis
- Investment Asset Management
- Property Development
- Investment Acquisitions
- Real Estate Law
- Residential Lending
