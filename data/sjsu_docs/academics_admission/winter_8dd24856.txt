SJSU Winter Session
SJSU offers winter session courses for currently enrolled SJSU students and non-SJSU students through Open University. Register today to earn college credits, save money on campus fees, and graduate faster.
Current SJSU Students
Currently enrolled SJSU students can register for Winter Session beginning a week before classes become available to the general public.
Open University
Former students, current students from another university, post-graduate students, and high school students can register for Winter Session without being formally accepted to a degree program through Open University.
Benefits of Winter Session
Save Money
Winter Session is the only term that doesn't charge campus fees, which results in a cost-savings of approximately $700.
Get Ahead
SJSU students who enroll in Winter Session courses tend to complete all of their required units faster.
No Formal Admission
Through Open University, non SJSU students can take courses without being formally admitted.
Earn Credits Quickly
During Winter Session, current students and Open University students can earn up to 4 credits in under 3 weeks.
Save Time
Lighten your spring course load by taking courses during Winter Session.
Course Convenience
Choose from 100+ online courses to best fit your needs.
