Title: Step To College (STC) Program
Type: HTML

The Step to College (STC) Program offers high school junior and senior students an
opportunity to register for available seats in lower division SJSU courses (lower
division courses are numbered in the 1-99 range). Learn about the different opportunities
in the Step to College Program below.
