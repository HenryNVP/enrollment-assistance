Parking Services Funding
Parking Services is 100% funded through the sale of parking permits. We receive no funding from the State of California's general fund.
Additionally, funds received from the sale of parking permits shall only be used for the maintenance, operation and acquisition of parking.
Parking citation revenue received shall only be used for alternative transportation programs, to mitigate the impact of traffic on the surrounding community and for the administration of the citation program itself.
Also, the State of California retains $12.50 from each paid citation for the operation of the courts and detention facilities.
