Get Involved
You have the power to impact the future!
By sharing your time and expertise with our staff, faculty and students, you empower inclusive innovators to shape the future. Our college’s mission is to prepare students for the evolving global job market. The most effective way to achieve this is by engaging alumni and supporters in the professional world. You can contribute in various ways: as a guest lecturer, student mentor, advisory group member, or by participating in real-world student projects. Please reach out any of the academic departments or program directors for more information on how to get involved.
