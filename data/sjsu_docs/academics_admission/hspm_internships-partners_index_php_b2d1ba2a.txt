Title: Internships and Industry Partners
Type: HTML

The Department of Hospitality, Tourism & Event Management is committed to collaborating
with notable hospitality companies that provide internships and job opportunities
for HTEM students while in school or upon graduation. The internship program is designed
to provide students with hands-on experience by applying business education and professional
hospitality management knowledge.
Please contact Dr. Tsu-Hong Yen, Chair and Internship Coordinator, for more information.
