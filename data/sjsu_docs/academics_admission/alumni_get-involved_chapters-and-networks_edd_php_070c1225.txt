EdD Leadership Alumni Network
About Us
The EdD Leadership Alumni Network was established by alumni of the SJSU Educational Leadership Doctoral (EdD) program to build relationships and provide opportunities for networking and professional development for educators and education leaders .
The vision of the EdD Leadership Alumni Network is centered on building and sustaining an equitable cradle to career educational system by supporting alumni.
Mission
The mission of the EdD Leadership Alumni Network is to cultivate a community of educators and education leaders to advocate for and work towards our vision of an equitable educational system.
