Recruitment Events
Do you have questions about SJSU and the application process? San José State University recruiters communicate regularly with international students like you from around the world. Answer your questions, receive feedback, and set yourself on the path to join us for your degree.
SJSU also has local and regional agents worldwide, and our authorized representatives attend international fairs and events ready to answer your questions.
Visit us online or attend an in-person info session to learn more about our campus, academic requirements, and application process.
If you still need more info, email us at global-students@sjsu.edu.
Webinar Series
The San José State international recruitment team hosts a series of virtual info sessions throughout the year. During these sessions, you’ll have the opportunity to engage with other prospective international students and learn more about our campus and an education at SJSU.
