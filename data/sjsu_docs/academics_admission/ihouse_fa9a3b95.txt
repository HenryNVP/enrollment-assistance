Title: Phyllis Simpkins International House
Type: HTML

At the International House, you'll live and study beside people from around the world. While sharing your culture, cuisine, and language with other residents, you will build friendships with them that will span across many continents and last the rest of your life. The ongoing support of the Simpkins family and Rotary International provides unique opportunities for you and other I-House residents to see and learn more than just what you come to study.
Special events, professional development, and other unique opportunities await you as a resident of the International House.
The history of the International House goes back fifty years. Read about its benefactors over the years and the people who manage it today.
