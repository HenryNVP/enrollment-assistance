The Interdisciplinary Engineering program in the Charles Davidson College of Engineering
prepares students with the skills and knowledge required for today’s existing and
emerging technologies. The program offers both a B.S. in Interdisciplinary Engineering
(BSIDE) and an M.S. in Engineering (MSE) degree. These programs provide students with
flexible curriculum that is truly interdisciplinary and not available through other
engineering programs in the college.
If you are interested in a much broader view of engineering than the traditional engineering
majors and the opportunity to work with other engineers across different disciplines
as well as working with non-engineers, then Interdisciplinary Engineering might be
your calling.
Students should review the Interdisciplinary Engineering curriculum for their catalog
year. Determine the elective courses required to meet their engineering career goals.
Make an appointment with an IDE major advisor regarding major course requirements
and an ESSC advisor for General Education course assistance.
Students should review information available for current MSE students that include
MSE program course requirements, study proposal and candidacy forms and MSE academic
advising. Included are FAQs.
