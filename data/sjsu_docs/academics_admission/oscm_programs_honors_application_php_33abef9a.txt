Application Process
Applying in Fall or Spring
SGIL students will normally be admitted to the program only in the fall semester. Applications to start the sequence in the spring semester are normally accepted only for students graduating in the next two semesters, and the admissions criteria are usually more strict than for students entering the program in the proper sequence.
For SGIL students, applications to start the sequence in the spring semester are normally accepted only for students graduating in the next two semesters, and the admissions criteria are usually more strict than for students entering the program in the proper sequence.
Most students are only eligible to apply to the program in April of their junior year. In past years, otherwise qualified students have been turned down because they missed this key deadline.
If you are not sure whether (or when) you are eligible to apply for the Sbona Honors Program, feel free to contact one of the honors faculty.
An information night for prospective students is held each semester. Find the date and time for the current semester.
