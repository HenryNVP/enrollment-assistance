Weeks of Welcome
Fall Weeks of Welcome (WOW) – is packed with 80+ worthwhile events all across SJSU, open to ALL currently enrolled students!
Whether you’re brand new to SJSU or a returning Spartan, WOW is your chance to get connected, make new friends, discover campus resources, and start the semester strong. The complete list of Spring WOW programs is available in the SJSU Sammy App.
Ask Me Tables
Starting a new semester can be overwhelming, but you don’t have to figure it out alone! Stop by Student Involvement’s “Ask Me” Table located on 7th Street Plaza for friendly, helpful guidance from fellow students and staff.
WOW Kick-Off
Start your semester with energy, excitement, and connection at the Weeks of Welcome Kick-Off! Join us at 7th Street Plaza for a vibrant, street festival-style event where campus resources meet student celebration.
Frequently Asked Questions
What is Weeks of Welcome?
WOW is a campus-wide celebration powered by the Spartan community!
Over 30 campus offices and departments come together to bring you WOW – a jam-packed series of events, activities, and resources during the first 5–6 weeks of every semester.
The goal - To give new and returning students a warm Spartan welcome and help them settle into life at SJSU. From finding your community to discovering campus resources, WOW is here to make your transition smooth, fun, and full of opportunities.
Around here, we call it WOW – and once you experience it, you’ll see why!
When is Weeks of Welcome?
WOW kicks off the new academic year with a bang, running from Thursday, January 2, to Saturday, February 28.
Do I have to be on campus for Weeks of Welcome?
Whether you’re on campus, tuning in from home, or somewhere in between, there’s a WOW event for you. With in-person, virtual, and hybrid experiences, everyone can join the fun — no matter where you’re living!
What is the Sammy App?
The San Jose State University app, also known as the Sammy App, brings services to your fingertips and enables you to connect with classmates and friends. Access events, calendars, contacts, maps, and more! Stay organized with the timetable function, which lets you save events, classes, and assignments.
We highly recommend that all SJSU students download the Sammy app - it’s a great way to connect with other students and learn about events and resources!
Is Weeks of Welcome required?
No, although it is encouraged! Attending WOW events is the best way to meet people and become better acquainted with campus and university resources—weeks of Welcome features key events that cater to our students’ diverse interests. Taking full advantage of WOW will help you get the best start at San Jose State University!
Where can I find information about Weeks of Welcome and event details?
The best places to find WOW information are this website and the Sammy App! Many offices and departments will also share information about their events and resources on social media; we invite you to browse and follow us on Instagram (@SJSUgetinvolved) to learn more! The website and the Sammy App will have the most up-to-date information and will also include links to virtual events.
