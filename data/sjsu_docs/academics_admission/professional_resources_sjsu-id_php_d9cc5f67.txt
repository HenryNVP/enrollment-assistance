SJSU Student ID
Students who attend classes with face to face instruction mode will need a Tower Card to access on-campus buildings. Please follow these instructions:
- Submit a photo online at https://myid.sjsu.edu/. Once you submit your photo, it should be available for printing within 48 hours of receiving your approval email.
- Please come to the Tower Card Window in the Student Services Center, 9th and San Fernando, to get your Tower Card. We're open Mon - Fri from 8:00am - 5:00pm. Join the Q-Less Queue when you get here.
Note that you must be authenticated to MySJSU to access the links above.
