Resources
We try to accommodate the basic needs of our students at SJSU, but we also recognize our capacity to serve everyone equitably. Below is a list of compiled resources we put together to assist if our services are not enough.
On-Campus
SJSU provides multiple resources to support your basic needs.
UndocuSpartan Student Resource Center
The USRC helps students connect with resources and services that are available to students who are undocumented.
Well-being@SJSU
Well-being@SJSU is a hub of resources and experiences to inspire and empower you to grow your well-being in eight different areas.
To support students in distress, view the SJSU Community Guide to discover several strategies for taking action when you observe changes in a student's well-being.
Financial Aid & Scholarship Office
The SJSU Financial Aid and Scholarships Office (FASO) can help you and get the money you need through grants, loans, scholarships, and work-study programs.
Associated Students Transportation Solutions
Transportation Solutions provides affordable and accessible alternatives and resources to driving on campus. Students commuting within San Jose and outside the county have a variety of options available at a discounted price.
Off-Campus Resources
Resources in the community to support your basic needs.
Second Harvest Food Bank
Use the Second Harvest Food Finder to find a South Bay food pantry near you.
Affordable Housing Finder
The United Effort's Affordable Housing Finder can help you find affordable housing based on preferences, like location, price, and accessibility.
Bill Wilson Center
Bill Wilson Center can help college students in Santa Clara County (up to the age 25) who lack a fixed, regular, and adequate nighttime residence.
Additional Resources
View a list of available resources in Santa Clara County, or visit 211.org to learn about resources across eight Bay Area counties.
