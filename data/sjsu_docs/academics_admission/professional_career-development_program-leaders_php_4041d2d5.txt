Course Proposal Steps
Four weeks prior to your course start date, follow these steps to request approvals for your course.
- Download the Course Proposal Form (PDF) with Adobe Reader.
- Fill out the form, save it as a PDF, and email it to ceuprograms@sjsu.edu. The "Policies and Process" section is located on page 2. By submitting a Course Proposal Form, you indicate agreement and adherence to these policies.
- Upon approval from Associate Vice Provost, Professional and Continuing Education (PaCE), your course will be set up and confirmed with a Class Roster and the instructions to submit the CEUs Requests will be sent to you by PaCE.
- A facilitator can send out the instructions for CEUs requests to students at any time.
- Upon class completion, the facilitator will reroute the Class Roster to ceuprograms@sjsu.edu for transcript verification purposes. Students can only request CEU transcripts when Class Rosters are sent back to SJSU after the class ends.
More questions: Please contact us at (408) 924-2655 or ceuprograms@sjsu.edu.
