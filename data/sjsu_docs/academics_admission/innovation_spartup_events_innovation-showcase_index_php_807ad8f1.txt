Innovation Showcase Exhibition Event
The Innovation Showcase is an open-air celebration of creativity, entrepreneurship, and bold problem-solving at San José State University. From 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., the campus transforms into a vibrant exhibition space where student innovators present their ideas, prototypes, ventures, and research to the community.
