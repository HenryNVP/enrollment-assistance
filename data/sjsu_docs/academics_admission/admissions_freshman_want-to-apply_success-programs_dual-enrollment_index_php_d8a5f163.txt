Title: Dual Enrollment
Type: HTML

SJSU Dual Enrollment Programs allow students to earn high school and college college
credit simultaneously for courses taken at or certified by San Jose State University.
Dual enrollment courses are similar to Advanced Placement courses; however, unlike
AP courses, dual enrollment courses do not culminate in a high stakes exam. Students
earn college credit through their course grades. For questions about the Dual Enrollment
program, please email us at dualenrollment@sjsu.edu.
Through STC Partnerships, SJSU enters into an agreement with a school or district
to reserve seats in SJSU classes for high school enrollment. Classes are taught at
SJSU (or online) by SJSU faculty.
