Title: Pre-Admissions Counseling
Type: HTML

Visit us at the Welcome Center for in-person pre-admissions counseling, Monday through
Friday, from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm.
Counseling is available on a drop-in basis—no appointment necessary! To speak with
a counselor virtually, simply join the virtual line below.
Are you a high school or college counselor interested in hosting an SJSU representative
at your school? Email us to inquire about scheduling a visit, and we’ll follow up
with our availability. We’re here to support you and your students every step of the
way.
