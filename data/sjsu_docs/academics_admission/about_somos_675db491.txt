Somos SJSU ("We Are SJSU")
HSI Initiatives
Digital Counter-Storying Faculty Learning Community
Somos SJSU is the foundation for a ground-breaking faculty professional development program that enhances students’ digital counter-storytelling skills.
Faculty across campus have developed partnerships to expand access to advanced degree programs for Latinx and other historically underserved students using the Somos SJSU framework.
