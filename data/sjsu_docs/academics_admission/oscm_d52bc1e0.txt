2025 OSCM Summit: Exploring the Future of AI and Careers in Operations & Supply Chain Management
In October 2025, SJSU Operations & Supply Chain Management Association (OSCMA) partnered with the SJSU Career Center and Fastenal for a one-day summit. This summit emphasized careers in Operations and Supply Chain Management and explored the future of AI.
