Financial Assistance
SJSU Cares assists with emergency financial support and guidance to help students manage unexpected expenses and basic needs. Our team offers direct assistance through emergency grants and resource coaching, while also serving as a bridge to other campus partners. We work with you to identify the right financial resources—whether within our office or across the university—to ensure you have the stability needed to stay in school.
