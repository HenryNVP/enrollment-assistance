Preparing for Your Visit
Get everything you need for a smooth and comfortable visit. Find details on parking options, campus directions, current weather advisories, and how to easily reschedule your visit if needed.
Directions and Parking
Please use the following address to guide you to the North Parking garage:
Student Services Center
60 S. 9th Street
San Jose, CA 95192
After exiting the North Parking garage, cross San Fernando St. and head onto campus. Once on the main campus, follow the greenbelt toward the Student Union and make the first right - you will end up between the Industrial Studies building and the Student Union. The tour will start at the Welcome Center, located on the north side of the Student Union.
Weather Advisory
To enjoy a comfortable campus tour, it’s important to be prepared for the weather.
San José State University is in Northern California, where conditions can change quickly. We recommend dressing in layers and bringing gear for both rain and sun, depending on the season.
Check the weather forecast before your visit to ensure you’re ready for whatever the day brings!
Need to Reschedule?
We know plans and interests can change. If needed, you can cancel individual visits or your entire itinerary using the link in your confirmation email.
For all other concerns please call our office at 408-924-2564.
