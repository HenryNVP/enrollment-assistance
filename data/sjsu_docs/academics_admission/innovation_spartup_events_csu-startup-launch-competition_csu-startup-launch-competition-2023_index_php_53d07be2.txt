Sunstone CSU Startup Launch Competition 2023
On May 5, 2023, 36 teams from 18 California State University campuses came together for the inaugural CSU-wide startup launch competition hosted at San José State University. Prizes of $25,000 for first place, $10,000 for second place and $5,000 for third place in each track were sponsored by Sunstone Management.
