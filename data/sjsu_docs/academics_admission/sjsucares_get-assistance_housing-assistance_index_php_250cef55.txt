Housing Assistance
SJSU Cares offers personalized support for students experiencing housing instability or a lack of a safe place to sleep. Receive one-on-one guidance to help you access short-term emergency housing, navigate the Rapid Rehousing Program in partnership with the Bill Wilson Center, and receive dedicated housing search assistance. Together, we work to identify stable living solutions and create an action plan that ensures your housing situation supports your academic success and overall well-being.
