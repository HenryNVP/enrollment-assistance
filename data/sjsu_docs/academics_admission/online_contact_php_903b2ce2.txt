Contact Us
Our enrollment counselors are here to support you throughout your inquiry, application, and admissions experience.
Call Us
Our enrollment counselors are available by phone Monday-Friday from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Pacific Time.
Email Us
Email us at your convenience and a member of our enrollment team will respond as soon as they are able to.
Request Info From Us
Fill out our form to receive more info about how SJSU Online can help you complete your bachelor's degree.
Schedule an Enrollment Counseling Appointment
Our SJSU Online Enrollment Counseling team is passionate about helping you continue your journey towards completing your bachelor's degree. Their collective experiences as transfer students, first-generation college students, graduates of the Cal State system, and admissions and enrollment counselors have provided them with a wealth of knowledge, insights, and empathy for potential SJSU Online students.
Speak with one of our enrollment counselors on the phone to discuss your goals, our degree-completion programs, and how to prepare to submit a successful application. Select your program of interest below to get started.
Santi Herrera Victor
Santi grew up in the East Bay—while he faced obstacles as a first-generation college student, he is proud to have accomplished his goal of graduating with his bachelor’s degree in Psychology. As an SJSU Online Enrollment Counselor, he is excited to learn about students’ lives and goals, and to support them in achieving those goals through a holistic approach by building relationships with students from the moment they are interested in an online program to the moment they receive their admissions. Outside of work, you can find Santi exploring and discovering new and exciting places to visit and eat.

Christa Cruz
Christa grew up on the East Side of San José and — while her educational journey has been full of challenges as a first generation and transfer student — she is incredibly proud to be a Latina college graduate. As an SJSU Online Enrollment Counselor, she understands that being a non-traditional student is difficult and loves guiding future students and watching them achieve their goals. Outside of work, she is pursuing her dream of completing a Master's Degree in Counseling and Guidance from SJSU.
Meet with ChristaReuel Evangelista
Reuel grew up in San José and — although life challenges caused him to take more than a decade to graduate from college — he persisted to complete his degree and would not be where he is today without it. As an SJSU Online Enrollment Counselor, he enjoys learning about each future students' story and building a rapport with them to make them feel comfortable and supported. He is also proud that students receive 100% support from when they start their application through graduation. Outside of work, Reuel loves spending time with his family and maltipoos, bowling, and cooking his parent's family recipes.
