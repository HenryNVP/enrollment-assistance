First-Time, First-Year Intent to Enroll
Once you are admitted, an electronic version of your admission materials will be posted to MySJSU and include information about completing your intent to enroll through the Next Steps portal.
The Next Steps Intent to Enroll process is a mandatory, multi-step process that includes choosing a mandatory orientation date and submitting the nonrefundable Enrollment Deposit. Completing this process lets us know you plan to attend SJSU.
On the Next Steps website, you will be able to:
- Submit your Intent to Enroll
- Acknowledge your academic plan
- Acknowledge Immunization Requirements
- See which orientation dates are available
- Register for mandatory orientation
- Pay the $250 nonrefundable Enrollment Deposit
- View your Preliminary Financial Aid Eligibility (if you completed the FAFSA/Dream Act Application)
- View your admission letter
If you have any questions about completing the Next Steps Intent to Enroll process prior to the deadline listed in MySJSU, contact nextsteps@sjsu.edu
