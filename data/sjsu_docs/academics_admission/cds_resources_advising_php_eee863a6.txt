Departmental Advising
Undergraduate and graduate students in Communicative Disorders and Sciences are strongly encouraged to meet with an academic advisor at least once per semester. These meetings will provide you with an opportunity to plan your courses and discuss your degree progress.
Bachelor of Science Degree Advisement
For questions about course planning, registration, student forms and petitions, SJSU resources, and anything else, please visit the Lurie College of Education Student Success Center (SSC).
Master of Science (M.S.) Degree Advisement
Graduate students are advised by tenured or tenure-track faculty advisors. Faculty advisors have regular office hours during Fall and Spring semesters of the academic year. Students are required to attend whole group advising sessions, and are encouraged to reach out for individualized support during office hours.
General programmatic questions, feedback, or concerns from undergraduate and graduate students also may be communicated directly to CDS Department Chair, Dr. Pei-Tzu Tsai, via email, office hour visits, or scheduled meetings.
