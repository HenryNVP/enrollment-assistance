ChAD Club
Welcome to the ChAD Club!
Participating in student organizations are a great way to make connections with other students, develop your leadership skills, apply your experiences outside of the classroom, and more. The Child & Adolescent Development (ChAD) club is open to all interested students, alumni, and faculty in the Child & Adolescent Development department. Our organization plans events that focus on personal, academic, and professional development as well as community outreach.
The ChAD Club has a bulletin board outside SH 201 where information about activities is posted. Join our ChAD Club Facebook page and follow our ChAD Club Instagram account (@chadclubofsjsu) for access to more opportunities.
ChAD Club Announcements
- Check our ChAD Club Facebook page and/or Instagram account for announcements about upcoming meetings and events.
- Bring your ideas for local events, (i.e., speakers, exhibits), resources, or potential service projects related to child and adolescent development to an upcoming meeting!
Contact Us
If you are unable to attend our meeting, but are still interested in participating in the ChAD Club, notify us via our ChAD Club Facebook page or ChAD Club Instagram account, or contact the ChAD department.
