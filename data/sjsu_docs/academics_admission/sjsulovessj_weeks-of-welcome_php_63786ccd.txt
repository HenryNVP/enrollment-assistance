Weeks of Welcome
Weeks of Welcome programs and events take place during the first few weeks of each semester. WOW welcomes new and returning students to campus, provides support for new students as they transition into SJSU. WOW also has some on and off-campus events and promotions at local businesses or sporting venues open to faculty, staff, and their friends and family.
Visit the Weeks of Welcome page and check out all of the free programs and events happening around campus and downtown San José.
