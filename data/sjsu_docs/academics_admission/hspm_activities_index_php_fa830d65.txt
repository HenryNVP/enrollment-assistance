Events and Activities
Hospitality Summit
The Hospitality Summit features panels ranging from career opportunities in hotels, food and beverage industries to event planning. Hear from industry experts and learn about the various career paths available in the hospitality industry. All core business functions are needed, so whether you're interested in management, sales, accounting/finance, marketing, or technology, this event is for you!
The Pineapple Forum
The Pineapple Forum is a series of presentations by hospitality professionals
and SJSU alumni to discuss the career opportunities in the hospitality, tourism and
event industry. All events are free and open to students.
You will learn about:
- Trends in the Hospitality, Tourism & Event Industry.
- Career opportunities and career path in the Hospitality, Tourism & Event
Industry. - Networking and connecting with experienced industry professionals and SJSU alumni.
- What you can do to prepare yourself while in school.
Register at https://forms.gle/xzDdVGQrAUt7ZXG18 to receive events updates.
