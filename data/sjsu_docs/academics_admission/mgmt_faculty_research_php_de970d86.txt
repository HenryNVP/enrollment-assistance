Research Highlights
At San Jose State University (SJSU), we place a high priority on research, with an annual investment of $60 million, ranking us among the top 200 schools nationally in terms of research spending. Our diverse community of students and faculty brings creativity and innovation to a broad spectrum of research areas.
In the School of Management, we intertwine research and teaching, committed to practical learning with real-world impact. We equip our students with the skills and knowledge to navigate the dynamic world of management, keeping our curriculum updated with the latest industry trends.
Our research emphasizes management research priorities such as strategic management, organizational behavior, human resource management, and operations management. Concurrently, we focus on HR priorities such as leader effectiveness, change management, and the future of work, aiming to develop critical skills that are highly valued by HR leaders.
