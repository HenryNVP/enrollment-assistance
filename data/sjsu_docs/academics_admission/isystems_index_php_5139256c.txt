This is MIS. A great career. Totally dynamic. Totally flexible. Totally YOU!
The Management Information Systems (MIS) Concentration in the School of Information Systems & Technology at SJSU combines business and technology–exactly what Silicon Valley is all about. Our program will train you to deliver the information solutions organizations need to meet their goals. That's why our graduates get amazing jobs at great places like Google, EY, PwC, Cisco, Apple, and eBay.
MIS students take the same core business courses as other business students but also get a great technical foundation that prepares them to apply cutting-edge technologies to create real value for today's organizations!
