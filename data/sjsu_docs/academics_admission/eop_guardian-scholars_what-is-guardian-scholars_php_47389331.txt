Title: Guardian Scholars Program
Type: HTML

If you've been in the foster care system, have been a ward of the court, or have experienced a related situation, we have extra resources and help you can use while you're a student here at SJSU. Check your eligibility and let us know if you'd like to learn more.
Resources available to Guardian Scholars
Financial support
Grants, scholarships, and fee waivers are available specifically for students like you. Let us help you find ways to help pay for college.
Study space and social lounge
Besides ready access to helpful supplies like testing material, hygiene items, snacks and free printing, you can find community with other scholars and our staff.
Guidance during your college journey
Take advantage of coaching meant to help you develop skills and succeed not just while you're here at SJSU, but wherever your degree ends up taking you.
