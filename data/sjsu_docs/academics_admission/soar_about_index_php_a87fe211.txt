The Office of Undergraduate Admissions and Outreach assists prospective students and
their families with the admission processing requirements and the subsequent steps
for admission to San José State University for both domestic and international applicants.
SOAR counselors/recruiters consistently visit high schools and community colleges
in the local area and throughout the Greater Bay Area. We attend college fairs and
education based outreach events, conduct presentations and build relationships with
community partners and educators locally, throughout California and in select target
states all throughout the country.
