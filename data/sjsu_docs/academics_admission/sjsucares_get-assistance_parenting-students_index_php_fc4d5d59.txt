Parenting Student Resources
Academic & Enrollment Support
Early Registration for Student Parents
Take advantage of early registration to coordinate your academic schedule with childcare
and family commitments if you provide more than half of the financial support for
at least one dependent child under the age of 18.
Pregnancy Accommodations
Request temporary academic adjustments through the AEC if you have documented pregnancy-related
limitations to ensure that medical or physical requirements do not hinder your participation
in your degree program.
Childcare & Family Services
A.S. Child Development Center
Explore high-quality, on-campus childcare and early childhood education options, including
potential subsidized care and financial assistance programs like CCAMPIS to help reduce
costs for eligible student parents.
Health, Wellness & Lactation
Student Wellness Center
Access primary care, mental health services, a low-cost pharmacy, and the Wellness
Lounge to help you maintain your well-being.
Lactation Accommodations
Coordinate directly with your faculty or the Office for Title IX and Equal Opportunity
to secure reasonable break times and academic flexibility so you can focus on your
studies while caring for your nursing needs.
Lactation Room at the Gender Equity Center
Stop by the Gender Equity Center in the Student Union to utilize their welcoming and
private lactation space designed specifically to support the needs of parenting students.
Off-Campus Resources
FindHelp.org
Explore a wide range of free or reduced-cost local programs, including food, housing,
diapers, and healthcare. Utilize this easy tool that will help you connect with the
essential services you need.
