Finance Lecturer, SJSU
Debbie Abbott has over thirty years of experience in marketing and finance with leading
Silicon Valley companies. She is currently a finance lecturer at SJSU and a board
member for the City of San Jose’s Retirement System. Her most recent marketing role
was as the Director of Content and Campaigns for Flex. She leveraged her social media
network to land that role. She believes networking through social media is an essential
tool for all job seekers. “It’s not just what you know, it’s who you know.”

at Xerox for ten years where she was recognized for her financial forecasting and
planning. Then she made a career change into marketing and has held senior marketing
management at Flex, Cisco, Xerox and more. She also did marketing strategy consulting
for several leading Silicon Valley companies.

Director, Food Safety & Regulatory Compliance, Weee!

navigating complex industries, leading cross-functional teams, and building resilient
systems that create both societal impact and business value.
Product Marketing Leader, Cisco

professionals and sharing her knowledge to empower others.
