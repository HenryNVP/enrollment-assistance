MS in Speech-Language PathologyContinuing Education Units
Continuing Education Units, or CEUs, are awarded by universities, and accredited training providers for the successful completion of non-credit programs and courses. At PaCE, we partner with a variety of organizations to provide CEUs training to improve the knowledge and skills of working adults.
