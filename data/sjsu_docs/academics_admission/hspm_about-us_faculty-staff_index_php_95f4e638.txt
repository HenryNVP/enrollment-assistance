Faculty and Staff
The Department of Hospitality, Tourism and Event Management is comprised of a team of full-time faculty, part-time lecturers, and administrative staff. Together they bring cutting edge theoretical and practical skills, knowledge, and experiences to deliver extraordinary classroom and field experiences to prepare students for their transition from college to their careers.
