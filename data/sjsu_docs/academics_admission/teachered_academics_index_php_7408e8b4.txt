Academics
We prepare transformative educators to teach at the elementary, middle, and high school levels through our credential programs. These programs are post-baccalaureate, offered at the graduate level, and are accredited by the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing (CCTC).
Our primary academic opportunities include a multiple subject (elementary education) or single subject (secondary education) credential, a credential plus a Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT) degree or, for experienced teachers, a Reading and Literacy Leadership Specialist Credential program with a Master of Arts in Multilingual and Multicultural Literacy Education (MMLE) degree.
