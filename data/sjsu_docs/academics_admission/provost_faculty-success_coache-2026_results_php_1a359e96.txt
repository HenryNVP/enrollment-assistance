Results and Reports
This page serves as the central location for COACHE survey results at San José State University. Here you will find:
- Summary reports and materials from the current COACHE administration (as available);
- Links to previous COACHE survey results, including the first administration in 2019; and
- High-level findings intended to support campus dialogue and understanding.
Official COACHE reports and data files are archived and stewarded by Institutional Research and Strategic Analytics.
COACHE results are not endpoints; they are starting points for reflection and conversation. Faculty, academic leaders, and governance bodies use these findings to identify strengths, surface challenges, and consider priorities for improvement in alignment with SJSU’s strategic goals.
