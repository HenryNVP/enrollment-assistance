Video Tours
Explore SJSU from Anywhere
Take a virtual video stroll through San José State University and get a closer look at campus life. Explore the videos below to discover various campus locations and learn more about what makes SJSU unique.
University Housing Tour
Explore San José State University’s student housing options from wherever you are with our virtual tour. Get an inside look at both on-campus and nearby off-campus residences, including apartment-style living, plus the amenities and support services available to students.
Student Union Tour
Join Sanreet, an SJSU Student Ambassador, for a tour of the Student Union. Discover the services, study spots, and favorite hangouts that make it a popular destination on campus.
King Library Tour
The Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Library provides study areas, research assistance, 3D printing, and student computing services. Take a video tour to explore even more resources available to you.
Spartan Recreation & Aquatics Center (SRAC) Tour
Discover the Spartan Recreation and Aquatic Center (SRAC), a state-of-the-art facility equipped to help you achieve your fitness goals. Enjoy 20,000 sq. ft. of cardio and strength equipment, three full-court gyms, an indoor 1/8-mile track, a climbing wall, a 50-meter lap pool, and a recreation pool with a sundeck.
Hammer Theatre Tour
Take a video tour of the Hammer Theatre and experience the excitement of live performances by SJSU artists as well as national and international acts.
