Naming Opportunities
Athletics / South Campus Capital Projects
The South Campus Master Plan addresses and benefits all San Jose State sports teams. From brand new stadiums for baseball and softball, a golf complex, tennis, beach volleyball and soccer facilities, and locker rooms, team and coaching spaces, modernizing South Campus will help student-athletes excel in competition and beyond.
