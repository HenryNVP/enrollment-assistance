Whether you already live and work in the Silicon Valley/Bay Area, or you’ve always wanted to, our main campus puts you in the middle of all the exciting action. We’re located right in downtown San Jose, where you’ll find all the amenities of a big city in a small city setting. Concerts, sports events, plays, museums, casual and fine dining – everything is nearby and easily accessible.
San José is midway between San Francisco (an hour to the north) and from the Santa Cruz Mountains (less than an hour to the south), with plenty of public transportation options, including to all points up and down Silicon Valley. Explore San José and Silicon valley.
