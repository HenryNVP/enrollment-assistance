Alumni and Friends
To all our Alumni and Friends: We welcome you back!
To our graduates, it was wonderful having you as a student – now even better having you as a valued alumnus or alumna who is taking advantage of new and exciting opportunities in your life. Its great being out in the world of global work. No more late nights studying, papers, quizzes, midterms and finals…
And, to our friends, thank you for supporting the next generation of SISTech professionals. They appreciate your enthusiastic involvement in their future.
Get Involved
We are always looking for Alumni and Friends as guest speakers – a great way to give back to young eager SISTech students. Reach out to your favorite professor or the Department Chair to let them know you are interested. Don’t have the time, consider becoming a Donor - invest in our college and its students.
Practicum Projects
The best sources for our projects are from alumni and friends of SJSU. We appreciate the time and commitment you provide to help make our student’s education an outstanding experience.
If you have a potential project, would like to be a guest speaker, or would like to donate, please do not hesitate the contact the Project Coordinator. We would love to hear from you.
