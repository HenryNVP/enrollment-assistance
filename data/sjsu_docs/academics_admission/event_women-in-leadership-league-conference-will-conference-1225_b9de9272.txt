View mapHosted by SJSU’s Lucas College and Graduate School of Business, the purpose of SJSU’s Women in Leadership League (WILL) Conference is to bring together top Silicon Valley leaders, alumni, and students to offer insights on career journeys upon graduation. C-suite leaders and mentors share inspiring experiences, practical advice, and strategies, while offering leadership perspectives, and opportunities for networking with high level executives in an empowering and supportive environment.
