The College is dedicated to maintaining its accreditation with AACSB International. First accredited by AACSB International in 1967, the college has undertaken a sustained effort to maintain accreditation via continuously improving our programs and college management processes. The College will continue to track performance to ensure that we maintain the high standards required of accredited schools. This site will act as an information clearinghouse to ensure efficient communication of accreditation documents relevant to our continuous improvement efforts.
