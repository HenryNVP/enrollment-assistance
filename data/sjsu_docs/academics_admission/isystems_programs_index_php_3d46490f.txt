Welcome
Dr. David Czerwinski, Interim School Director
Do you enjoy learning and applying your technical know-how? Then great news, there are many career options for you in Management Information Systems (MIS)! Are you passionate about technology but prefer the business side of things? The MIS field needs you too! That’s because MIS professionals are specialists in technology AND business who integrate the best of both to solve problems and create value for businesses, non-profits, and our community.
So join our MIS concentration in the Lucas College and Graduate School of Business at SJSU and learn how you can craft a meaningful career you’ll love using your unique talents and some super cool tech!
It's fun, it's flexible, and it’s in demand! That’s MIS!
We have a great program here at San Jose State and we would love to have you join us!
