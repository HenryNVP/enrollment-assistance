Chair's Message
During student transition to the university life and to reach graduation, our students partake in many hospitality management job opportunities in the heart of Silicon Valley and in the broader BayArea. There are also several opportunities to participate widely in the community and university. Many of our students are highly active in numerous student groups, for example, the Hospitality Financial and Technology Professionals (HFTP) and the Pebble Beach Special Event Management Team (SEMT).
Our curriculum equips students with the knowledge and necessary skills to perform professionally and become leaders in the Hospitality Industry. With a degree in Hospitality, Tourism and Event Management, our alumni have a wide range of hospitality related careers including Hotel/Resort General Manager, Financial Controller, Catering Coordinator, Meeting Planner, Sales Director, Marketing Manager, Conference/Tradeshow Manager, Banquet and Events Coordinator, Restaurant Manager, etc. Our alumni are among the most prominent professionals in the Hospitality Industry.
We are very proud and honored of our department’s history and achievement, as you explore more, you will understand why. Please contact the Department of Hospitality, Tourism and Event Management with any questions or concerns.
Best Regards,
Dr. Tsu-Hong Yen
Department Chair
