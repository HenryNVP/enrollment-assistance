College of the Humanities and the Arts
Our College leverages the power of the Humanities and the Arts to ask the most important questions of our time. As our faculty and students grow and achieve success, they become equipped to answer these questions with confidence and skill. Located in the heart of Silicon Valley, our values of curiosity, creativity, and compassion are vital to the DNA of technological innovation. The future of California starts here, at the College of Humanities and Arts at San José State University.
Support
We are dedicated to providing our students a fulfilling collegiate experience—employing excellent faculty, crafting rewarding curriculum, and fostering student-led extracurricular opportunities.
Connections
We are proud to be a part of San José—amplifying community voices in the arts and beyond, fostering networks with local businesses, and providing high-quality programming and events for all to enjoy.
Innovation
We are invested in the future—exploring new ways of learning that are hands-on and practical, building facilities with cutting-edge technologies, and supporting our academic centers that research and redefine the canon.
