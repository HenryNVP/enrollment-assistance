Study and Collaboration Room
The Study and Collaboration (SAC) room is a communal space that is available during the academic year for currently registered Lurie College students and is located in Sweeney Hall 446. The space provides access to a computer, printer, and copy machine. Technical assistance is available by appointment at 408-924-3640. Students must use their SJSU Tower Cards to access the room.
