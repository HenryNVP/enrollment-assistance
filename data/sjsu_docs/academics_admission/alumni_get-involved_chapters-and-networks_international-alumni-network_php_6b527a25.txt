International Alumni Network
About Us
Founded in 2021, and officially recognized in 2022, the International Alumni Network (IAN) cultivates international partnerships, professional development, and social interactions for San Jose State University Alumni and friends of the university through in-person and virtual networking events, professional opportunities, and worldwide friendships. Our goal is to engage and partner with SJSU alumni to share global interests, further professional careers, enhance university internationalization efforts and provide scholarships to support international experiences.
Mission
To engage and connect SJSU alumni and friends of the university who have global interests and experiences they would like to share with the broader SJSU community.

Nondiscrimination statement
We serve all students equitably and inclusively and foster an environment rooted in respect, collaboration, and trust. At San José State University, all students, regardless of race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, national origin, or background, are welcome to engage with the International Alumni network, learn with and from others, and be in community. We serve all students equitably and inclusively and foster an environment rooted in respect, collaboration, and trust. Through our programs and events, we foster understanding of International experiences and create opportunities for all Spartans to learn, belong, and succeed together.
